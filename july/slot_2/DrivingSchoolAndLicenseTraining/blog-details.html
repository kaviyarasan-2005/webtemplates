Navigating Roundabouts Safely: Lane Rules & Yielding

James Miller, Senior ADI Coach Published July 14, 2026 4 MIN READ TELEMETRY

Understanding Roundabout Traffic Priorities

Roundabouts are designed to maintain continuous traffic flow and minimize severe T-junction broadside collisions. For learner drivers, however, roundabouts can feel daunting due to fast-approaching traffic and prompt lane decisions.

KEY INSTRUCTOR TAKEAWAY

"Patience is your best defensive tool at a roundabout. Never force entry or cut off circulating vehicles inside multi-lane turns."

1. Approaching & Lane Selection

As you approach, reduce your speed to gear 2 and select your entry lane early. General lane rules: for turning left (1st exit), select the left lane; for going straight (2nd exit), select the left or middle lane; for turning right (3rd exit or full circle), use the right-hand lane.

2. Yielding & Priority Rules

Vehicles already circulating inside the roundabout have absolute right-of-way. Yield to traffic approaching from your immediate right, and wait for a safe gap before entering smoothly.
