Our Story

Establishment

Educating Safe Drivers Since 2014

DRIV was founded with a single mission: to make our roads safer by transforming how people learn to drive. Over the past twelve years, we've grown from a small family operation into a leading national academy, training over 15,000 confident drivers.

Our approach combines modern teaching methodologies, flexible digital booking platforms, and high-safety dual-control vehicles to guarantee a stress-free educational experience.

Safe Training Cars

Our Modern Safety Fleet

We train you using modern, environmentally clean cars equipped with state of the art safety controls.

Dual-Control Training Cars

Every single training car in our fleet features professional dual-control mechanical brake systems. This guarantees that your instructor can intervene instantly in hazardous situations, ensuring 100% peace of mind during your first hours on busy public roads.

Modern Safety Features

Our vehicles are equipped with multiple airbags, anti-lock braking systems (ABS), lane departure warning alerts, electronic stability controls, and blind-spot mirrors. You'll learn driving maneuvers on the safest cars available today.
