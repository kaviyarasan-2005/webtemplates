Our Journey

Transforming How People Learn to Drive

DRIV was established in 2014 with a singular commitment: eliminating driver anxiety and lowering road collision rates through telemetry-backed instruction methods.

What started as a single dual-control vehicle operation has expanded into a premier national driving academy with over 50 vetted ADI coaches and specialized vehicle fleets.

Connected directly to the passenger cabin floor, our dual mechanical foot pedals allow your certified coach to instantly take control of braking and clutch functions in any critical situation.
