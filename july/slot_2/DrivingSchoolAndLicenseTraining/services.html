Transmission Options

Choose Your Transmission

We provide specialized vehicles for both manual shift and automatic preferences.

Manual Transmission Training

Mastering a manual car gives you complete mechanical control over your vehicle. You will learn precise clutch control, smooth gear selection timing, hill start techniques, and save fuel. Passing on manual grants you a license to drive both manual and automatic vehicles.

Automatic Transmission Training

Automatic driving is highly popular, simple to learn, and allows you to focus 100% of your attention on the road, hazards, and steering. With no clutch pedal or gear changes to worry about, students typically require fewer hours of instruction to pass their practical test.
