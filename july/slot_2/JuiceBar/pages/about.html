Our Humble Beginning

From a Garage Kitchen to Your Favourite Cafe

Juicy was founded in 2018 by lifelong friends Maya Chen and David Torres, who believed that truly healthy living should be accessible, delicious, and joyful. Starting with just a cold-press juicer and a weekend farmer's market stall, they quickly built a loyal following of health-conscious customers who couldn't get enough of their vibrant blends.

By 2020, demand grew so rapidly that they opened their first brick-and-mortar cafe in Freshville, California. Today, Juicy serves over a thousand customers daily, offers full-scale office catering services, and has become a cornerstone of the local wellness community.

  • All produce is sourced from certified organic local farms
  • Zero preservatives, no added sugars, no artificial flavours
  • Eco-friendly, compostable packaging across all products
  • Nutritionist-designed blends for maximum health benefit

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

2018

Founded at the Farmer's Market

Maya and David launch Juicy with a single cold-press juicer at the Freshville Sunday Market.

2019

First 1,000 Customers

Word-of-mouth growth brings us our first thousand loyal customers within 12 months of operation.

2020

Brick-and-Mortar Opens

We open our first cafe on Green Valley Road, featuring a full menu of juices, smoothies, and acai bowls.

Office Catering Launch

We expand into corporate catering, serving over 50 companies in our first quarter alone.

Juicy is officially certified organic, reinforcing our commitment to purity and natural ingredients.
