ATELIER KNOWLEDGE BASE

Luxury Textile Care Cheat Sheet

Worsted Wool

Dry Clean Only

Brush after every wear with a natural horsehair brush to release dust and restore fiber loft.

Pure Silk

Steam / Cool Iron

Never spray water directly while ironing. Store in breathable cotton garment bags away from light.

Linen Blends

Damp Pressing

Embrace natural wrinkles as character. Press while slightly damp with high steam for crisp lapels.

Cashmere & Alpaca

Flat Drying

Allow 24 hours of rest between wears. Fold horizontally rather than hanging to maintain shoulder shape.
