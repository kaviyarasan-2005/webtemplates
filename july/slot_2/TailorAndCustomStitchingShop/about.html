Our Mission

To deliver impeccably tailored garments that celebrate individuality, combining traditional techniques with contemporary design to create clothing that fits perfectly and lasts beautifully.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in custom tailoring, where every client feels confident, elegant, and uniquely dressed for every occasion in their life.

Our History

Founded in 1998 by master tailor Elena Vasquez, SEWIT began as a small alteration shop in downtown New York. Elena's unwavering commitment to precision and her eye for design quickly earned a loyal following. Today, SEWIT has grown into a full-service tailoring house, serving thousands of satisfied clients while maintaining the intimate, personal touch that started it all.
