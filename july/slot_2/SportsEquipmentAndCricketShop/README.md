# PLAY — Sports Equipment & Cricket Shop

PLAY is a premium multi-page static website designed with a championship-winning aesthetic, tailored for professional team custom kit orders, retail catalog browsing, and sports guide reviews.

## Vibe & Design System
- **Theme Color Palette (Obsidian & Gold):**
  - Primary (Indigo-Black): `#1A1A2E`
  - Secondary (Parchment White): `#F8F4EF`
  - Accent (Championship Gold): `#FFB400`
- **Dark Mode:** Activates clean deep gray surface highlights (`#1A1A1A`) against solid true black backgrounds (`#0A0A0A`).
- **Typography:** Display title headers in *Oswald* with clean body sections formatted in *Inter*.
- **Direction Toggle:** Built-in LTR/RTL support shifting grids, alignment directions, menus, and sidebars on click.

## File Structure
- `index.html` — General services landing page
- `home-alt.html` — Niche builder & custom wear config portal
- `about.html` — Mission values, process steps, count metrics & partner brands
- `services.html` — Interactive equipment finder, pricing accordions & feature grids
- `service-details.html` — Full custom sportswear kit features & pricing table details
- `pricing.html` — Price comparisons & discount metrics
- `blog.html` — Articles grid with live category searches
- `blog-details.html` — Reading panels, author card & comment validator
- `contact.html` — Direct support email forms, hours list & Google Maps iframe
- `404.html` — Clean boundaries bounce ball page
- `coming-soon.html` — Target launch timer subscription landing
- `assets/` — Complete dark style sheets, logic controls & layout assets
