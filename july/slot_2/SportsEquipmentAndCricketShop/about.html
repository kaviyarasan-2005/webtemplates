Our Story

From a small cricket shop to Chennai's most trusted sports equipment destination. Every champion has an origin story.

Since 2015

Built by Athletes, For Athletes

Our Mission

To make professional-grade sports equipment accessible to every athlete, from weekend warriors to championship contenders. We believe that the right gear can transform performance.

Our Vision

To become India's leading sports equipment and team customization brand, known for quality, innovation, and service that goes beyond the sale.

Our History

Founded in 2015 by former cricket captain Vikram Rajan, PLAY started as a small cricket bat workshop in Anna Nagar, Chennai. Today, we serve over 500 teams and 10,000 individual athletes across India, offering equipment for cricket, football, badminton, and gym fitness.
