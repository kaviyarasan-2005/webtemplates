Established 2015

Built by Athletes, For Athletes

Founded in 2015 by former first-class cricketer Vikram Rajan, APEX began with a simple mission: to ensure local sports academies had access to authentic, hand-selected English Willow equipment.

Over the past decade, we've expanded our reach to outfit over 500 sports teams across cricket, football, badminton, and fitness disciplines with customized kits and pro-tier gear.
