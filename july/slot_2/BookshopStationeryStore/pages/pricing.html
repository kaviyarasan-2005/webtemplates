Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my membership at any time?
Yes, all memberships are billed monthly and can be canceled at any time through your online account portal.
Does the discount apply to rare books?
The percentage discounts apply to all standard retail books and stationery. Rare and antiquarian books have a separate pricing structure, but Curator members do receive waived sourcing fees.
How do I access VIP events?
Curator members will receive private email invitations to VIP events with RSVP links. Registration is on a first-come, first-served basis.
