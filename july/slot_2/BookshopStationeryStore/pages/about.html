Our Story

A Haven for Readers Since 1998

Folio started as a small corner shop dedicated to rare first editions. Over the decades, we've grown into a sanctuary that celebrates not just the words on a page, but the art of the physical book and the tools used to write them.

We believe in the tactile experience of reading—the smell of old paper, the crispness of a new binding, and the smooth flow of ink from a fountain pen.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the Folio story.

1998 - The Beginning

Opened our first 500 sq ft location focusing purely on vintage paperbacks.

2005 - Expansion

Moved to our current flagship location on Literary Lane and introduced our first line of artisan stationery.

2018 - The Reading Lounge

Added the cafe and lounge space to host our expanding roster of community events.

Giving Back

We are proud to donate 5% of all our annual profits to local literacy programs. In addition, we host free weekly reading sessions for children and supply three local schools with discounted educational materials.
