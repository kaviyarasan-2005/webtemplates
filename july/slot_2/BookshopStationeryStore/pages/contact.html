Common Questions

Do you buy used books?
We only purchase rare, antiquarian, or first edition books. We do not currently buy general used paperbacks. Please email our curation team with photos of your items before bringing them into the store.
What is your return policy?
Books and stationery can be returned within 14 days of purchase with a receipt, provided they are in their original, unused condition. Custom engraved items are final sale.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship globally! Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on the weight of the items and the destination country.
