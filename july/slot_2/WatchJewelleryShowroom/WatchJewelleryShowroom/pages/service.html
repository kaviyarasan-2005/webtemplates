The At-Home Care Guide

Keep your collection pristine between visits to our atelier with these expert care tips.

  • Diamonds: Clean weekly with a soft brush and mild soap solution.
  • Mechanical Watches: Keep away from strong magnetic fields.
  • Gold & Platinum: Store in individual pouches to prevent scratching.
