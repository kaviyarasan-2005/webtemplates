Quick Answers

Everything you need to know before visiting our flagship showroom.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are always welcome during regular hours, but private appointments are recommended for bespoke design consultations.

Is valet parking available?

Yes, we offer complimentary private valet parking directly at our 123 Luxury Avenue entrance for all showroom guests.

Are items insured in transit?

Every timepiece and jewelry piece sent for service or valuation is 100% insured under our armored transport policy.

Do you provide valuation papers?

All custom pieces and estate watches include certified appraisal documentation for insurance and authentication purposes.
