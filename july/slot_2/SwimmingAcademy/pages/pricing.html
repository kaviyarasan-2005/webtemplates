Pricing Frequently Asked Questions

No! We believe in 100% transparent pricing with zero sign-up fees.

Yes, you can upgrade at any time and pay only the pro-rated difference.

We accept major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and automatic bank drafts.
