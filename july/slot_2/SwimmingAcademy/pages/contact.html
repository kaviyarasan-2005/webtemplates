Getting Here

Directions & Parking Info

We are conveniently located in central Hydro City, easily accessible by car or public transit.

Free On-Site Parking

150 covered parking bays available on-site for all Hydro members & visitors.

Subway Access

3-minute walk from Central Aquatic Station (Exit B) directly to our reception.

Bus Routes

City Bus Lines 12, 45, and 88 stop right outside our main entrance gates.
