Step-By-Step Growth

Swimmer Skill Level Journey

LEVEL 01

Water Acclimation

Submersion, breath control, floating, and basic kicking mechanics.

LEVEL 02

Stroke Fundamentals

Freestyle side breathing, backstroke arm rotation, and streamline glide.

LEVEL 03

Stroke Mastery

Breaststroke timing, butterfly kick rhythms, flip turns, and diving.

LEVEL 04

Endurance & Speed

Pacing sets, interval training, split timing, and squad competitions.

Program Frequently Asked Questions

What is the instructor-to-student ratio?
For Toddler & Junior classes, max 1:5. For Adult classes, max 1:8.
Are parents allowed on deck during lessons?
Yes, we have a dedicated glass seating gallery overlooking the main pool.
What if my child is afraid of water?
Our coaches use gentle play-based desensitization techniques to build confidence at their pace.
Can I switch batch timings mid-month?
Yes, subject to slot availability with 48 hours notice.
