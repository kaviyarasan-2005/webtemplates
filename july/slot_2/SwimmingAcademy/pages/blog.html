Quick Advice

Coach's Corner: Bite-Sized Tips

Breathe Out Underwater

Never hold your breath underwater. Exhale continuously through your nose for smoother side breathing.

High Elbow Catch

Keep your elbow higher than your wrist during the pull phase to maximize propulsion leverage.

Kick From the Hips

Avoid bending your knees excessively. A strong flutter kick originates right from your hip flexors.

Hydrate On Deck

You sweat while swimming! Keep a water bottle poolside to prevent muscle cramping.
