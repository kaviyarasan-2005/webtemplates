Frequently Asked Questions

How long does engraving take?

Standard engraving adds 1-2 business days to your order processing time. Premium tier members get priority processing (same-day if ordered before 12 PM).

Can I bring my own item to be engraved?

Currently, we only offer engraving and monogramming services on items purchased directly through our store to ensure material compatibility.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide! Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on the destination and package weight.

What is your return policy on personalized items?

Due to the custom nature of personalized items, we cannot accept returns unless the item is defective or we made an error in the customization.
