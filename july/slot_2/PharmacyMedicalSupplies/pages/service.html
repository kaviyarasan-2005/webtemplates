FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our services? Find quick answers below.

How do I upload a prescription?
Take a clear photo of your doctor's prescription showing the doctor's name, date, patient details, and medicine names. Upload it during checkout or use our Contact page's prescription form. Our pharmacist verifies it within 15–30 minutes.
Are all medicines genuine and FDA-approved?
Yes. Every product in our inventory is sourced directly from licensed manufacturers and authorized distributors. We are an FDA-licensed pharmacy and conduct regular audits to ensure 100% authenticity of all medicines.
Can I rent medical equipment for home use?
Absolutely. We offer weekly and monthly rental plans for oxygen concentrators, nebulizers, hospital beds, wheelchairs, crutches, and more. Equipment is sanitized and delivered with setup included. Call us to check availability in your area.
How does the cold chain delivery work for insulin?
Temperature-sensitive medicines like insulin are stored in our WHO-GDP certified cold storage at 2–8°C. During delivery, they are placed in insulated pouches with gel packs to maintain the correct temperature throughout transit. You'll receive a temperature log with your delivery.
Can I get a vaccine at the pharmacy walk-in?
Yes, walk-ins are welcome at our physical pharmacy location during store hours. You can also book an appointment online for flu shots, COVID boosters, travel vaccines, and routine immunizations. A digital vaccination certificate is issued immediately after.
