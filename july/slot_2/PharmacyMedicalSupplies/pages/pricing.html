FAQ

Billing & Plan Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing and membership plans.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?
Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account dashboard. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all paid plans.
Are medicine discounts applied automatically?
Yes, member discounts (10% for Standard, 20% for Premium) are automatically applied at checkout for all eligible products. Some manufacturer-controlled pricing items may have limited discounts, which will be clearly marked.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and ACH bank transfers. All transactions are processed securely via Stripe. Cash on delivery is available for product orders only.
Can I add family members to my plan?
Standard plan supports up to 4 family members, and Premium supports up to 10. Each family member gets their own health profile, prescription history, and delivery address. Family members share the plan benefits but have individual medication records.
Is there a corporate or business plan?
Yes, we offer custom enterprise plans for businesses, clinics, and care homes with bulk medicine procurement, dedicated account managers, and volume discounts. Contact our business team at business@medicareplus.com for a custom quote.
