Our Story

Two Decades of Trusted Care

Founded in 2005 by Dr. David Patel, a clinical pharmacist with 20 years of hospital experience, MediCare Plus began as a single neighborhood pharmacy committed to one principle: every patient deserves access to genuine, affordable medicines.

Today, we operate a state-of-the-art licensed pharmacy with an online platform serving 200+ cities, a team of 150+ certified pharmacists, and a catalog of over 15,000 products spanning medicines, nutraceuticals, baby care, and medical devices.

Our cold-chain logistics, prescription verification system, and 24/7 pharmacist helpline set us apart as India's most trusted healthcare delivery platform.
