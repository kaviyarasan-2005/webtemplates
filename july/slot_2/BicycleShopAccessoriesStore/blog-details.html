How to Choose the Right Carbon Wheels in 2025

By Marco Rossi • Published June 15, 2025 • 6 min read

In This Article

  • 2. Rim Depth vs Handling
  • 3. Tubeless vs Clincher
  • 4. 2025 Master Recommendations
Share Article
Twitter Facebook Copy Link

1. Why Upgrade to Carbon Wheels?

Upgrading from factory alloy wheels to carbon rims is the single most noticeable performance enhancement you can make to your bicycle. Carbon wheels reduce rotating mass by up to 500 grams, dramatically increasing acceleration out of corners and efficiency on steep climbs.

2. Rim Depth vs Handling Trade-Offs

Shallow rims (30–35mm) are lightweight, nimble, and unaffected by crosswinds — ideal for San Francisco's steep hills. Deep-section rims (50–65mm) offer superior aerodynamics on flat terrain but require confident handling in windy coastal gusts.

3. Tubeless vs Clincher Systems

Modern carbon wheels are optimized for tubeless setups. Running liquid sealant at lower pressures increases cornering grip while virtually eliminating pinch flats.

4. 2025 Master Recommendations

For all-around Bay Area riding, we recommend a 40mm to 45mm depth tubeless-ready carbon wheelset paired with 28mm tires.

Key Takeaways
  • Choose 35–45mm depth for hilly, windy terrain like the Bay Area.
  • Always check brake track warranty if using rim-brake carbon wheels.
  • Run tubeless tire pressure between 60–75 PSI depending on rider weight.
