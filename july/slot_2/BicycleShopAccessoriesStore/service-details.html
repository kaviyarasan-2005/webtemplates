Complete Repair & Tune-Up

Restore your bicycle to factory performance. Our certified mechanics disassemble, clean, adjust, and test every critical system using digital precision tools.

What Happens During Your Tune-Up

Whether you ride a modern carbon fiber road bike or a heavy-duty commuter e-bike, regular servicing prevents component failure and ensures butter-smooth shifting. Our workshop uses factory diagnostic software for electronic groupsets and precision torque wrenches on all carbon interfaces.

Diagnostic Checkpoint Highlights

  • Laser alignment check on derailleur hanger
  • Hydraulic fluid contamination & boiling point test
  • Ultrasonic solvent tank dip for chain & cassette
