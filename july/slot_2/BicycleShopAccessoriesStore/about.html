2010 — The Spark

From a Single Workbench in San Francisco

VELOX was born out of a small garage off Cycle Lane with three workstands and a simple mission: bring honest, race-grade bike mechanics to everyday commuters and competitive cyclists alike.

2017 — Expansion

Full-Service Showroom & Fitting Studio

We expanded into our current 4,000 sq ft flagship showroom, adding a state-of-the-art Retül 3D bike fitting studio and stocking custom wheelsets from worldwide master builders.

Today & Beyond

15+ Years of Cycling Excellence

Over 12,000 repairs and 4,800 custom builds later, VELOX remains fiercely independent, rider-owned, and obsessed with perfection on every single ride.

Quality Standard

Our 4-Step Workshop Standard

Every bike that enters VELOX undergoes rigorous 4-stage quality control.

01

Multi-Point Digital Assessment

Frame alignment check, torque verification, chain wear measurement, and computerized battery diagnostic for e-bikes & electronic shifting.

02

Root-Cause Ultrasonic Cleaning

Drivetrain parts submerged in ultrasonic solvent tanks to strip grit and restore factory efficiency before rebuild.

03

Torque-Specified Precision Rebuild

Reassembly using digital torque wrenches, fresh stainless cables, and high-performance synthetic lubricants.

04

Road Test & Master Sign-Off

Every serviced bike is test-ridden on San Francisco's hills by a lead technician and signed off before customer pickup.
