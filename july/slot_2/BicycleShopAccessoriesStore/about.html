Our Story

Home / About
Since 2009

Driven by a Love of Cycling

VELOX was founded in 2009 by lifelong cyclist Marco Rossi, a former pro racer who wanted to bring professional-level service to everyday riders. What started as a small repair shop in San Francisco's Mission District has grown into a full-service cycling destination.

Our Mission

To make premium cycling accessible to everyone — from beginners taking their first pedal strokes to competitive racers chasing podium finishes. Every customer deserves expert advice, quality products, and service that goes beyond expectations.

Our Vision

A city where cycling is the preferred mode of transportation and recreation, and every rider has access to the knowledge and equipment to ride safely and confidently.

How We Work

Our Workshop Process

Every bike that enters our workshop goes through a rigorous 4-step process to ensure perfect results.

1

Assessment

We conduct a thorough inspection of your bike, documenting every issue and discussing your riding goals.

2

Diagnosis

Our technicians identify root causes — not just symptoms — using professional diagnostic tools.

3

Repair

Precision work using OEM or superior quality parts, with real-time updates sent to you throughout.

4

Quality Check

Every repaired bike is test-ridden and signed off by our head mechanic before returning to you.
