Service Packages

Common questions about our service offerings

What delivery areas do you cover?
We deliver within a 30-mile radius of Garden City, CA. This includes downtown, suburban neighborhoods, and surrounding towns. For locations outside our delivery zone, we partner with trusted courier services for an additional fee. Contact us for custom delivery arrangements.
What is your freshness guarantee?
We guarantee that every arrangement will stay fresh and beautiful for at least 7 days with proper care. If your flowers wilt before that, we'll replace them free of charge. We include care instructions with every delivery and our team is always available for flower care tips.
Can I customize my arrangement completely?
Absolutely! We offer full customization options. Choose your preferred flowers, color palette, arrangement style, vase or wrapping, and add personal touches like cards or ribbons. For wedding and event packages, we offer complimentary design consultations to bring your vision to life.
Do you offer corporate accounts?
Yes! We offer corporate accounts with special pricing, regular delivery schedules, and dedicated account managers. Whether you need weekly office arrangements, event florals, or client gifts, we tailor our services to meet your business needs. Contact us for a custom corporate quote.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How far in advance should I order for a wedding?
We recommend booking wedding florals at least 3-6 months in advance to ensure availability of your preferred flowers and our designer's schedule. For peak season (May-October), booking 6-12 months ahead is ideal.
Do you offer flower subscriptions?
Yes! We offer Weekly, Bi-Weekly, and Monthly subscription plans. Each delivery features a fresh seasonal arrangement. You can pause, skip, or cancel anytime. Visit our Pricing page for plan details.
What happens if the flowers I want aren't in season?
Our florists will suggest beautiful alternatives that capture the same color palette and aesthetic you desire. We always discuss substitutions with you before proceeding, ensuring you're delighted with the final arrangement.
Can I add extras like chocolates or a vase?
Absolutely! We offer a range of add-ons including premium vases, artisan chocolates, greeting cards, balloons, and plush teddy bears. Browse our Add-Ons section on the Pricing page for all available options.
Do you accommodate allergies or sensitivities?
Yes, we take allergies seriously. Let us know about any sensitivities during your order and we'll create a beautiful arrangement that avoids specific flowers or pollen-heavy varieties. We offer many low-allergen options.
