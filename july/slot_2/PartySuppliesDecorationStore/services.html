Service FAQ

Everything You Need To Know

Quick answers to common questions about booking and setup.

How long does setup take?

Setup typically takes 2 to 4 hours depending on package size. Our team arrives early to finish before guests arrive.

Are teardown services included?

Yes! All full-service packages include post-event teardown and prop pickup within 1 hour after your party ends.

Can you do outdoor setups?

Absolutely. We use outdoor weighted bases, weather anchors, and UV-resistant balloons for sun and wind stability.

Do I get to keep the balloons?

Yes! All balloons are yours to keep or pop at the end. Rental prop frames (arch hoops, neon signs) are collected during teardown.

What deposit is needed?

A 30% deposit holds your date. The remaining balance is paid 7 days prior to event setup.

Can I customize balloon colors?

100%! We match any color swatch, Pinterest board, or brand hex code at no extra charge.
