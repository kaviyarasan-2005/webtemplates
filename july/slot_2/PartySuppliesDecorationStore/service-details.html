Organic Balloon Garlands & Walls

Service Description

Our organic balloon installation service turns blank venue walls, staircases, and entrances into mesmerizing visual statements. Every garland is built on-site by our certified sculptors using multi-sized (5", 11", 16", 24") latex balloons for a natural, flowing texture.

Technical Specifications

  • Lifespan: Indoor 3–5 days / Outdoor 12–24 hours
  • Material: 100% Biodegradable natural latex
  • Setup Time: 2.5 hours on-site
