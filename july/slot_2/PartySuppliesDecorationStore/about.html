Our Journey Since 2016

We Don't Just Decorate.
We Create Memories.

From a small garage studio to over 5,000 decorated venues across the region — FESTA is dedicated to turning your happiest moments into stunning visual reality.

Work With Our Team
Chapter 1 · The Beginning

Born from Passion & Balloons

FESTA was founded in 2016 by Elena Morales, who started crafting bespoke party backdrops and balloon arrangements out of her garage. What began as a passion project for friends quickly exploded through word of mouth into a full-scale event styling company.

Chapter 2 · The Philosophy

No Cookie-Cutter Setups, Ever

We believe every event has a unique personality. Whether it's a child's 1st birthday or a 25th wedding anniversary, we never reuse template designs. Every balloon color blend, floral arch, and lighting accent is customized to match your exact vision.

Chapter 3 · The Future

Innovating The Art of Celebration

Today, our team includes certified balloon sculptors, floral artisans, and lighting technicians. We continue to push creative boundaries using eco-friendly latex balloons and sustainable prop materials.
