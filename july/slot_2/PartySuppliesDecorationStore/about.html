About FESTA

Our Story

Born from a love of celebration, FESTA has been creating magical decoration experiences since 2016.

Where It All Began

From a Garage Studio to 5,000+ Events

FESTA was founded in 2016 by lifelong party enthusiast Elena Morales, who started decorating friends' birthdays out of her garage. What began as a passion project quickly grew into the most trusted event decoration service in the region.

Our Mission

To make every celebration — regardless of budget or scale — feel extraordinary. We believe every person deserves a beautifully decorated event that reflects their personality and creates lasting memories.

Our Vision

To become the most beloved decoration brand across North America, known for our creativity, reliability, and the joy we bring to every event we touch.
