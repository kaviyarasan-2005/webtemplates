About ColorCraft

Delivering exceptional painting services with integrity, expertise, and a passion for color for over 15 years.

Our Purpose

Transforming Spaces, Elevating Lives

Founded in 2009, ColorCraft Painting began with a small crew and a simple belief: every home and business deserves a flawless finish applied by professionals who truly care about their craft.

Over the years, we have grown into a premier painting contractor, known for our meticulous prep work, premium materials, and unparalleled customer service.

Our Journey

15 Years of Growing Together

Humble Beginnings

David Miller started ColorCraft with just a van, two ladders, and a commitment to quality interior painting.

2014

Expanding to Exteriors

We expanded our services to include exterior house painting and deck staining, growing our crew to 15 painters.

2019

Launched our dedicated commercial painting division, partnering with property managers and local businesses.

2024
