About Pulse

Serving India's health needs with integrity, expertise, and genuine care for over 15 years.

Our Purpose

Making Quality Healthcare Accessible to Every Indian

Founded in 2009 by Dr. Arun Mehta, Pulse began with a single pharmacy in New Delhi with a simple belief: every person deserves access to genuine medicines and professional healthcare guidance, regardless of their location or income.

Today, we operate a network of 120+ stores across 28 cities, serve 500,000+ customers monthly through our digital platform, and employ over 2,000 licensed pharmacists and healthcare professionals.

Our Journey

15 Years of Growing Together

2009

Founded in New Delhi

Dr. Arun Mehta opens the first Pulse pharmacy in Connaught Place, New Delhi with 2 staff members.

2014

10 Stores Milestone

Expanded to 10 stores across Delhi-NCR. Launched prescription home delivery — a first in the region.

2019

Digital Platform Launch

Launched the Pulse app and website, enabling online prescription uploads and doorstep delivery nationwide.

2024

100+ Stores Across India

Reached the milestone of 100 stores across 20 cities. Crossed 500,000 active customers on our digital platform.
