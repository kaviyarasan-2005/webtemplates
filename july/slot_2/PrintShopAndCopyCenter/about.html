Who We Are

A Legacy of Quality Printing

Founded in 2011, INKA began as a small neighborhood print shop with a single offset press and a big dream. Today, we operate a state-of-the-art facility equipped with the latest digital and offset printing technology.

Our journey has been fueled by an unwavering commitment to quality, precision, and customer satisfaction. We believe every print job, whether a set of business cards or a large-format banner campaign, deserves the same attention to detail.
