Business Cards

Premium business cards that make lasting first impressions

Service Overview

Crafted for Impact

Your business card is often the first tangible representation of your brand. At INKA, we craft premium business cards that combine exceptional design with superior printing techniques.

Choose from a wide range of paper stocks, finishes, and special effects including gold foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV coating, and letterpress printing. Each card is precision-cut and quality-inspected.
