Fabric Care Protocol

What is
Dry Cleaning?

Dry cleaning uses specialized non-aqueous solvents to dissolve oils and lift stains from fabrics that cannot tolerate water without shrinking, warping, or fading.

Our process utilizes GreenEarth® silicone-based solvents — 100% odorless, non-toxic, and gentle on delicate silks, woolens, and tailored suits.
