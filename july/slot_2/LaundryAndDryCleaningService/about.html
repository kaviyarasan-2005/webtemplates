Who We Are

A Decade of
Fresh Beginnings.

WASH was founded in 2016 with a simple belief: everyone deserves professionally cleaned clothes without the hassle. What started as a small neighborhood laundry service has grown into a trusted partner for thousands of households and businesses.

Our commitment to eco-friendly practices sets us apart. We use biodegradable detergents, energy-efficient machines, and water-recycling systems to minimize our environmental footprint.

Workflow

Our 5-Stage Quality Journey.

1

Intake & Fabric Sorting

Got Questions?

Everything You Need to Know.

Simply choose your preferred pickup date and 2-hour window online. Our driver arrives with reusable WASH laundry bags, collects your clothes, and delivers them back freshly cleaned within 24 hours.

Delicates are hand-inspected, tagged with fabric care barcodes, and cleaned using zero-tension organic hydro-wash methods with pH-balanced detergents to preserve texture and color vibrancy.

If you're ever unsatisfied with the press, cleanliness, or fragrance of any garment, notify us within 24 hours and we will pick up and re-process the item free of charge.
