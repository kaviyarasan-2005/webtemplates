Our Story

Founded with a mission to make professional laundry care accessible, convenient, and eco-friendly for everyone.

Who We Are

A Decade of Fresh Beginnings

WASH was founded in 2016 with a simple belief: everyone deserves professionally cleaned clothes without the hassle. What started as a small neighborhood laundry service has grown into a trusted partner for thousands of households and businesses.

Our commitment to eco-friendly practices sets us apart. We use biodegradable detergents, energy-efficient machines, and water-recycling systems to minimize our environmental footprint while delivering spotless results.

Today, WASH serves over 10,000 customers across the city, processing more than 50,000 orders annually. Our customer-first approach and attention to detail have earned us a 99% satisfaction rate.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver freshness with care and convenience. We aim to transform the laundry experience by combining professional quality with modern technology and eco-conscious practices, making clean clothes accessible to every household and business.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted laundry partner in every neighborhood. We envision a world where professional garment care is effortless, sustainable, and available at your doorstep, setting the gold standard for the industry.

Our Facility

State-of-the-Art Equipment

We invest in the best technology to deliver superior cleaning results.

Industrial Washing

Our commercial-grade washing machines handle up to 100 lbs per cycle with precise temperature and cycle control. Advanced water filtration ensures no detergent residue, while our smart load-balancing system protects delicate fabrics.

Every machine is serviced weekly to maintain peak performance and hygiene standards.

Eco-Friendly Drying

Our energy-efficient dryers use heat-pump technology to reduce energy consumption by 50%. For delicate items, we offer natural air-drying in our climate-controlled facility, preserving fabric integrity and extending garment life.

We also use biodegradable fabric softeners and fragrance-free options for sensitive skin.
