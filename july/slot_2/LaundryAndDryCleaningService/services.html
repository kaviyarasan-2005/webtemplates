Fabric Care

Specialized Treatment

Different fabrics require different care. We tailor our approach for optimal results.

Gentle Care for Delicates

Silk, cashmere, lace, and other delicate fabrics require special attention. Our trained technicians use pH-balanced detergents and low-agitation cycles to preserve the integrity of fine materials.

Each delicate garment is inspected individually, hand-treated where necessary, and air-dried to prevent shrinkage or damage.

Heavy-Duty Cleaning

Workwear, denim, towels, and heavily soiled items get our industrial-strength treatment. High-temperature washes with commercial-grade detergents tackle the toughest stains and odors.

Our reinforced agitation cycles and specialized pre-soak treatments ensure even the most stubborn grime is removed without compromising fabric strength.
