The Ultimate Guide to
Reading Laundry Care Labels

By Marcus Rivera, Head of Operations

Those tiny symbols stitched into your clothing tags are a complete language — and once you learn to read them, you'll never damage another garment in the wash again.

Why Care Labels Matter

Every clothing manufacturer is required to include a care label that tells you exactly how to wash, dry, and iron the garment. These instructions are based on the fabric composition, dye sensitivity, and construction of the item. Ignoring them is the number one reason clothes shrink, fade, or lose their shape prematurely.

The international care label system uses five types of symbols, each representing a different aspect of garment care. Let's decode them one by one.

Key Takeaway

When in doubt, wash cold. Lower temperatures protect fabric integrity and save energy.

The Five Care Symbols

1. Basin Symbol: Washing temperature & cycle type.
2. Square Symbol: Tumble dry vs flat air dry.
3. Iron Symbol: Heat level & steam allowance.
4. Circle Symbol: Professional dry cleaning.
5. Triangle Symbol: Bleach tolerance.
