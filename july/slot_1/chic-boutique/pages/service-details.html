About This Service

The Experience

Our bespoke evening wear service is the pinnacle of the CHIC atelier experience. Working with the finest silks, duchess satins, and hand-finished lace from our Parisian and Italian suppliers, our master tailor creates garments that are as unique as the women who wear them.

Every creation begins with an intimate consultation at our Mayfair atelier, where we explore your vision, occasion, and aesthetic preferences. We then produce detailed sketches and source fabric samples for your approval before a single stitch is sewn.

The fitting process consists of three appointments spanning four to six weeks, ensuring absolute perfection of drape, silhouette, and structure. Our seamstresses bring decades of couture experience to every garment, working with techniques passed down through generations of Parisian ateliers.

The result is not merely a dress — it is a garment you will keep, cherish, and pass down. A CHIC creation is an heirloom from the moment it is finished.
