Our Process

How It Works

A considered, step-by-step journey from first conversation to finished wardrobe.

  1. Consult

    Share your vision with our lead stylist in an intimate, unhurried consultation. No judgement, no pressure.

  2. Curate

    We handpick pieces tailored precisely to your personality, occasion, budget, and colour palette.

  3. Fit

    Our master tailors ensure every single garment fits to absolute perfection before it leaves the atelier.

  4. Deliver

    Your curated wardrobe, wrapped in CHIC tissue and delivered to your door or collected in-store.
