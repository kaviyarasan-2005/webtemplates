Fully Bespoke

Custom Creations

Our bespoke commission service is the ultimate expression of the CHIC philosophy. We collaborate with each client to bring their vision to life — from initial sketch to the final stitch of the finished garment.

Fabrics are sourced directly from our European partners in Milan, Paris, and Lyon — silks, duchess satins, fine wools, and hand-finished lace. The production process spans 4–6 weeks with unlimited revisions until perfection is achieved.

  • European fabric sourcing
  • 4–6 week production timeline
  • Unlimited revision consultations
  • Full satisfaction guarantee
