The Art of Capsule Dressing:
Building a Wardrobe That Works

June 12, 2026 Style Tips 8 min read

Article Content

The capsule wardrobe concept dates to 1970s London, when boutique owner Susie Faux coined the term to describe a small collection of essential items that would never go out of fashion. Today, in an era of algorithmic trend cycles and overnight micro-trends, the capsule wardrobe feels more radical — and more necessary — than ever.

The Foundation Pieces

Every capsule wardrobe begins with five to eight foundational pieces — the garments you reach for instinctively. At CHIC, we recommend starting with: a perfectly fitted white shirt, a tailored blazer in a neutral tone, one pair of well-cut trousers, a versatile midi dress, and a quality knit or cashmere jumper.

"The best wardrobe is not the largest one — it is the one where every single piece belongs there."

— Isabelle Marchetti, CHIC Founder

Quality Over Quantity

The mathematics of a capsule wardrobe is simple: ten high-quality pieces that work together create far more outfit possibilities than fifty fast-fashion items that compete with each other. We call this the multiplier effect — each new addition must work with at least three existing pieces to earn its place.

Colour as Architecture

The most effective capsule wardrobes operate within a defined colour palette — typically two neutrals, one accent, and one print that ties them together. At CHIC, we help clients identify their instinctive palette during a style consultation, then build outward from that foundation.

Building a capsule wardrobe is not a weekend project — it is an ongoing, evolving practice of self-knowledge through clothing. Start small, choose well, and let every piece earn its place in your life.
