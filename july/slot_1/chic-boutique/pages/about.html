Our Story

Our Journey

From Passion to Boutique

Founded in 2012 in the heart of London, CHIC began as a small atelier with a singular vision — to make extraordinary fashion accessible to those who appreciate the art of dressing. Our founder, Isabelle Marchetti, had spent a decade in Parisian haute couture before bringing her expertise and eye for detail to London's thriving fashion scene.

Today, CHIC stands as one of London's most beloved independent boutiques, recognised for our uncompromising standards and deeply personal approach to style. Every garment on our rails has been handpicked, every fabric scrutinised, every stitch considered.
