Bulk Order FAQ

What is the minimum quantity for a bulk discount?

Bulk discounts start at 25 items. You save 10% on 25–99 items, 20% on 100–499, and 30% on 500+ items.

Can I mix different products in a bulk order?

Yes! The discount is based on total item count, not per-product. You can mix notebooks, pens, and any other items.

How long does bulk delivery take?

Standard bulk orders are processed within 48 hours. Orders of 500+ items may take 3–5 business days. Express options are available for 500+ orders.

Do you offer custom branding / logo printing?

Yes! For orders of 500+ items, we offer custom logo printing on notebooks and bags. Contact us for a custom quote.
