Our Service

Custom Printing Service

Bring your brand to life with our professional custom printing solutions.

At DESK, we offer end-to-end custom printing services designed for schools, businesses, and organizations of every size. From branded notebooks and pens to tote bags and folders, our state-of-the-art equipment delivers crisp, vibrant results on a wide range of materials.

Whether you need 50 units for a small team or 10,000 for a corporate event, we scale our process to meet your requirements without compromising on quality.

Capabilities

We support screen printing, pad printing, digital UV printing, and foil stamping. Each technique is selected based on your material, budget, and design complexity to ensure the best possible outcome.

Materials

Print on paper, cardboard, cotton, polyester, canvas, bamboo, and select plastics. Our team helps you choose the right substrate for durability and print quality.

Minimum Orders & Turnaround

Minimum order quantity starts at just 25 units. Standard turnaround is 7–10 business days. Need it sooner? Rush production is available for most products at an additional fee.
