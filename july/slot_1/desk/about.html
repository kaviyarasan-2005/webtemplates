About DESK

From a Small Shop to a Nationwide Supplier

DESK was founded in 2010 with a simple vision: to make high-quality stationery and school supplies accessible to everyone. What started as a small family-run shop in Knowledge City has grown into one of the region's most trusted suppliers of office essentials, art materials, and educational tools.

Our founder, inspired by the struggles students and teachers faced in finding reliable, affordable supplies, set out to build a company that puts quality and customer care first. Over the years, we've expanded our catalog to thousands of products, partnered with schools and businesses across the country, and stayed true to our original promise — delivering exceptional value without compromise.

Today, DESK serves over 5,000 schools, 2,000 offices, and thousands of individual customers who trust us to keep their desks stocked with the best.

Milestones

Our Journey

Key moments that shaped DESK into the company we are today.

2010

Founded

DESK opens its first small shop in Knowledge City, driven by a passion for quality stationery and a belief that great tools spark great ideas.

2013

First School Contract

Secured our first major partnership with the Knowledge City School District, supplying 200 classrooms with essential stationery for the entire academic year.

2018

Online Launch

Launched our e-commerce platform, bringing DESK's full catalog online and reaching customers across the country for the first time.

2022

Eco-Initiative

Introduced our green product line — 100% recycled notebooks, biodegradable pens, and plastic-free packaging — setting a new standard for sustainable stationery.
