A Recipe Born From Passion

YUMZ was started by the Rossi family in a small kitchen on Flavor Street. What began as a humble family recipe — a sourdough-based pizza dough passed down through three generations — quickly became the neighbourhood's best-kept secret.

Today, we serve hundreds of happy customers every day, staying true to our roots: real ingredients, no shortcuts, and food made with genuine care.
