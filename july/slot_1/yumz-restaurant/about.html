About YUMZ

A family story, a community institution, and a passion for great food.

Est. 2008

Where It All Began

It started with a simple dream: Marco Rossi, a first-generation immigrant from Naples, wanted to share the food of his childhood with his new home in Foodville. In 2008, he opened YUMZ in a tiny 400 sq ft space on Flavor Street with a single pizza oven and a handwritten menu.

2008

Marco opens YUMZ with 6 tables and a dream. Day one saw a 3-hour queue around the block.

2012

YUMZ expands to a 60-seat dining room and launches takeaway service. Named "Best Pizza in Foodville" by the Local Food Guide.

2016

Marco's daughter Sofia joins as Head Chef, introducing the now-legendary smash burger and pasta range.

2020

YUMZ launches delivery services and feeds the community during lockdown through its "Pizza for Heroes" initiative — donating 5,000+ meals to frontline workers.

2024

YUMZ celebrates 15 years, serves its 50,000th customer, and opens a second location across town.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision

Every pizza, every burger, every side dish is made with a deeper purpose in mind.

Our Mission

To craft food with genuine care — using premium, responsibly-sourced ingredients that bring joy to every table. We exist to make great food accessible, consistent, and worth coming back for, every single time.

  • Premium, responsibly-sourced ingredients
  • Consistent quality every day
  • A warm, welcoming experience

Our Vision

To become Foodville's most beloved restaurant — a community cornerstone where families gather, milestones are celebrated, and every meal is a memory in the making. We aim to prove that fast food can be genuinely great food.

  • Community-first restaurant model
  • Expanding without compromising quality
  • Fast food raised to fine-food standards

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These aren't just words on a wall — they define every decision we make at YUMZ.

Fresh Ingredients

We source locally where possible and refuse to compromise on quality. If it isn't fresh, it isn't on the menu.

Fast Service

Your time matters. Whether dine-in or delivery, we work to get your food to you hot, fast, and exactly as ordered.

Customer First

Every decision starts with the customer. From menu design to packaging, your experience is our priority.

Community Love

We give back. From school sponsorships to local events and charity meals — YUMZ is proud to be part of Foodville.
