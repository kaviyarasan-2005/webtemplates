How It All Started

It all began when our founders couldn't find a single place that offered truly premium, vetted pet products. Every trip to the store was a compromise on quality or selection.

We decided to build what we were looking for: a destination that curates the world's best pet brands, rigorously tests every product, and genuinely cares about animal welfare.
