About PAWS

A team of passionate animal lovers dedicated to providing the best care for your beloved companions since 2015.

Our Story

From a Small Shop to a Pet Paradise

PAWS started as a small pet supply shop founded by two lifelong animal lovers who noticed a gap in quality pet care services in the community.

Over the years, we grew into a full-service pet care destination, offering grooming, training, boarding, and a curated selection of premium pet products. Our commitment has always been the same: treat every pet as if they were our own.

Today, PAWS serves hundreds of happy pet families, and we continue to expand our services while maintaining the personal touch that made us who we are.
