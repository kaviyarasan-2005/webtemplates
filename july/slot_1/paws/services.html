Most dogs benefit from grooming every 4-6 weeks, while cats generally need grooming every 6-8 weeks. Breeds with longer or thicker coats may need more frequent visits. We can recommend a schedule based on your pet's specific needs.

Dogs must have current Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella vaccinations. Cats need Rabies and FVRCP. We require proof of vaccination from a licensed veterinarian at least 48 hours before boarding.
