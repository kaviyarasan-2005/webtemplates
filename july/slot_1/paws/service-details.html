About This Service

Expert Pet Grooming

Our certified groomers bring years of experience and a genuine love for animals to every session. We use only premium, pet-safe products and state-of-the-art equipment.

Whether your pet needs a quick bath or a full breed-specific styling, our team delivers a stress-free, enjoyable experience. We handle each pet with gentle care and patience.

Every grooming session includes a complimentary health check where we look for skin issues, parasites, lumps, or any concerns worth mentioning to your veterinarian.

Grooming FAQ

Basic grooming takes 1-2 hours, Full Groom takes 2-3 hours, and Premium Spa takes 3-4 hours. Duration depends on your pet's size, coat condition, and behavior.

We use only premium, pet-safe, hypoallergenic products. Our shampoos are sulfate-free and our conditioners are enriched with natural oils. We can accommodate sensitive skin upon request.
