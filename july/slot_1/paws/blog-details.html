Summer Pet Care: Keep Your Pets Cool and Safe

July 10, 2026 • 8 min read

Summer brings longer days, outdoor adventures, and quality time with your pets. But the heat also brings risks that every pet owner should understand. Here is your comprehensive guide to keeping your furry friends safe during the hottest months.

Hydration Is Key

Always ensure fresh, clean water is available to your pets. On hot days, consider adding ice cubes to their water bowl. When外出 with your pet, carry a portable water bottle and collapsible bowl. Signs of dehydration include excessive panting, dry gums, and lethargy.

Recognizing Heatstroke

Heatstroke is a life-threatening emergency. Watch for these symptoms:

  • Excessive panting and drooling
  • Bright red or purple gums
  • Vomiting or diarrhea
  • Stumbling or disorientation
  • Collapse or seizure

If you suspect heatstroke, move your pet to shade immediately, apply cool (not cold) water to their body, and contact your veterinarian right away.

Safe Walking Schedule

The pavement can reach dangerous temperatures even on mild days. Place the back of your hand on the ground for seven seconds. If it is too hot for you, it is too hot for your pet's paws.

Walk your pets during the cooler parts of the day: early morning before 8 AM or evening after 6 PM. During peak heat, keep exercise to a minimum and opt for indoor play instead.

Grooming for Summer

Regular grooming helps regulate your pet's body temperature. Consider a shorter summer cut for long-haired breeds, but never shave double-coated dogs as their coat actually insulates against heat. Regular brushing removes loose undercoat and improves air circulation through the fur.

Our summer grooming packages include a cooling treatment and lightweight styling that helps your pet stay comfortable all season long.

Sun Protection

Pets can get sunburned too, especially those with light-colored fur and exposed skin areas like noses and ears. Use pet-safe sunscreen on vulnerable areas and provide shaded resting spots in your yard. Limit outdoor time during peak UV hours between 10 AM and 4 PM.

Summary

Summer should be enjoyable for both you and your pets. By staying hydrated, avoiding peak heat, maintaining regular grooming, and knowing the signs of heat-related illness, you can ensure a safe and fun season for everyone. When in doubt, schedule a wellness check at PAWS and let our veterinary team help you prepare.
