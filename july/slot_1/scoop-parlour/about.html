Est. 2010

From a Dream to Your Cone

It all began in 2010 in a small sun-drenched kitchen in Napa Valley. Elena and Marco Rossi, inspired by years of summers spent in their grandmothers' kitchens in Naples, decided to bring the authentic Italian gelato tradition to California. They started with a single hand-cranked machine, six flavours, and an unshakeable belief that great ice cream could change someone's day.

Our philosophy has never changed: use only locally sourced, seasonal ingredients. No artificial colours, no preservatives, no compromises.

What began as a weekend farmers' market stall quickly became the neighbourhood's most-talked-about treat. By 2013, SCOOP opened its first brick-and-mortar parlour. Today, we serve thousands of happy customers each week, yet every batch is still crafted with the exact same hands-on care as that very first scoop.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Three principles guide every decision we make, from ingredient sourcing to customer service.

Quality First

We never compromise on ingredient quality. Every component — from the dairy to the tiniest sprinkle — is personally tested by our chefs before it reaches your cone.

Community Driven

We partner with local farms, sponsor neighbourhood events, and donate 1% of every sale to food banks. Our success belongs to the community that built us.

Sustainability

Compostable packaging, zero food waste policy, and a solar-powered kitchen. We're committed to leaving the planet sweeter than we found it.

Craftsmanship

How We Craft Every Scoop

From farm to cone, our meticulous process ensures perfection in every bite.

Select

We handpick only the finest seasonal fruits, premium dairy, and artisan flavourings every single morning.

Mix

Our master chefs combine ingredients using time-tested family recipes enriched by innovative modern techniques.

Churn

Every fresh batch is slow-churned in our Italian machines to achieve the perfect dense, creamy texture.

Serve

Each scoop is served with care, topped to perfection, and handed to you with a genuine smile.
