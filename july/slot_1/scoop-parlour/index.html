Our Promise

Made with Love

Every ingredient, every scoop, every smile — crafted with genuine care for you and our community.

Fresh Ingredients

We source local dairy and seasonal fruits every morning from trusted farms within 50 miles of our parlour.

Handcrafted Daily

Every batch is churned fresh in our open kitchen, never pre-frozen. What you taste today was made this morning.

Natural Colors

No artificial dyes here. Our vivid pinks, purples and greens come entirely from real fruits and vegetables.
