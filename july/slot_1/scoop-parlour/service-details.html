FAQs

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your SCOOP event.

Do you provide vegan and gluten-free options?
Yes! We offer a full range of certified vegan sorbets and gluten-free flavours clearly labelled for your guests. Please inform us of all dietary requirements at the time of booking so we can ensure a safe and delicious experience for everyone.
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance for standard events. For weddings and large events (100+ guests), we advise 6–8 weeks notice to allow for custom flavour development, logistics planning, and securing your preferred date.
What is your service area?
We currently serve within a 50-mile radius from our main parlour in Dessert District, CA. Extended travel can be arranged for an additional logistics fee. Please contact us directly to confirm availability for your location.
Can I customise the flavour selection?
Absolutely! Our event coordinator will work closely with you to curate the perfect flavour lineup for your guests, dietary needs, and event theme. For Premium packages, we can even develop a bespoke signature flavour just for your event.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 7 or more days before the event receive a full refund of any deposit paid. Cancellations within 7 days will receive a 50% credit applicable to a future booking. We understand life happens — please contact us as early as possible so we can work something out.
