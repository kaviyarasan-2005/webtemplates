Quick Answers

Common Questions

Do you take reservations?
We operate on a walk-in basis at our parlour — no table reservations needed. For private events and catering bookings, advance reservations are required and can be arranged via our catering page or by calling us directly.
Is there parking available?
Yes! There is free street parking along Sweet Lane and a public car park just half a block away on Dessert Drive. We also have secure bike racks right outside the front door for our cycling community.
Do you offer vegan and allergy-friendly options?
Absolutely. We have a dedicated vegan menu featuring 8+ dairy-free sorbets and plant-based ice creams. Full allergen information is displayed prominently at our counter and is available on request. Please ask a member of our team for assistance.
