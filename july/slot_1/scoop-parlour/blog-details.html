The Art of Waffle Cones

Elena Rossi July 8, 2025 Recipes

The History of the Waffle Cone

The waffle cone has a surprisingly contested history. The most popular story dates to the 1904 World's Fair in St. Louis, where an ice cream vendor and a nearby waffle seller combined forces out of necessity — and the rest is sweet history. What started as a practical solution to a shortage of dishes became an icon of summer joy the world over.

Our Handmade Process

The Batter Recipe

Our recipe contains just four honest ingredients: stone-milled flour, French unsalted butter, fine cane sugar, and pure Madagascar vanilla. We mix the batter fresh each morning and let it rest for exactly 45 minutes before pouring. No stabilisers, no artificial flavouring, no shortcuts.

The Rolling Technique

Every cone is rolled by hand within 20 seconds of leaving the iron, while the batter is still pliable and golden. Our staff train for two full weeks on this technique alone — the angle of the roll, the pressure of the hand, the speed of the wrap. It's a skill that takes months to truly master and years to perfect.

"A perfectly formed waffle cone takes three years to master, but one bite to fall in love with."
— Elena Rossi, Head Chef

Why Handmade Matters

Mass-produced cones use stabilisers, palm oil, and artificial vanilla flavouring to achieve consistency at scale. Ours use nothing but four honest ingredients, baked fresh every morning in small batches. The result is a cone that shatters with a satisfying crunch, perfumes the air with real vanilla, and elevates even the simplest scoop into something memorable.

Pairing Your Cone

The neutral sweetness of our classic waffle cone pairs beautifully with sharp, citrus, or tropical flavours — lemon curd, passionfruit, raspberry sorbet. For richer flavours like chocolate fudge or salted caramel, consider our darker, caramelised cone variant, which is available at the counter daily while supplies last.
