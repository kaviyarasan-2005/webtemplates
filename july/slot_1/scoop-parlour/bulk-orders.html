FAQs

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance for our Sweet Start and Premium Scoop packages. For the Grand Celebration, please allow at least 1 month to ensure full customization and availability.

Do you travel outside the city?
Yes! We serve events within a 30-mile radius. A travel fee may apply for distances beyond 15 miles. Contact us for a custom quote.

Can I customize the flavor labels?
Custom flavor labels are included with the Premium Scoop and Grand Celebration packages. For Sweet Start, this can be added as an optional extra.

What if my guest count changes?
We allow final guest count adjustments up to 48 hours before your event. Significant changes (more than 20%) may affect pricing. We'll always work with you to find a solution.
