Frequently Asked Questions

Do time bundles expire?

No, time bundles purchased in your account never expire. You can use them across multiple visits.

Can I share my bundle with a friend?

The 20-Hour Marathon Pack can be shared with up to 1 other person. Other bundles are strictly tied to a single account.

Is there an age limit for VR?

Players must be at least 8 years old. Players under 13 require a parent or guardian present in the lounge.
