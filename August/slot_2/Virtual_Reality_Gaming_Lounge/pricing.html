Help & Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing hours, memberships, and session policies.

Do time bundles ever expire?

No! Prepaid time bundles purchased in your player account never expire. You can consume them in 30-minute or 1-hour increments across as many visits as you like over weeks or months.

Can I share my hours or bundles with friends?

Yes! The Pro Pack (10 Hours) can be shared with 1 registered friend, and the Squad Marathon Pack (20 Hours) allows you to link up to 3 friends so your whole squad can draw from the same balance. Diamond Members can also bring 2 free guests every month.

How do monthly membership hours rollover work?

For Gold Members ($49/mo), unused monthly hours roll over to the next billing cycle up to a maximum bank of 4 hours. For Diamond VIP ($99/mo), all unused monthly hours roll over indefinitely with zero cap as long as your membership is active.

Can I switch games during my booked session?

Absolutely. You have unrestricted access to all 50+ games in our library. You can switch between games at any time through our custom VR headset dashboard or ask any floor VR Master for an instant recommendation.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Standard bookings can be rescheduled or cancelled for a 100% wallet credit up to 2 hours before your scheduled session. Diamond VIP members enjoy instant cancellation privileges with zero time penalty.
