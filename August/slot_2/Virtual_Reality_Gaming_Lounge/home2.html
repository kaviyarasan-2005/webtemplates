How It Works

We make your VR experience completely seamless. No prior gaming experience required — our team takes care of everything.

01

Pick Your Session

Book online to guarantee your preferred pod and time, or walk in spontaneously. Choose from 30, 60, or 90-minute blocks.

02

Gear Up & Fit

Our VR Masters guide you through custom lens adjustment, medical-grade hygiene liners, and an effortless 2-minute controls briefing.

03

Jump Into Worlds

Explore our full 50+ library. Want to switch from shooter to rhythm halfway through? One tap on your headset and you're in.

Chill & Compare

Relax at our neon lounge bar, sip craft sodas and cocktails, watch live spectator replays on 4K displays, and compare scores.
