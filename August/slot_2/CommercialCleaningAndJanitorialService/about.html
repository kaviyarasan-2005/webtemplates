Our Story

SPOT was founded by two facility managers who were exhausted by inconsistent vendors, broken promises, and cleaning crews who treated their clients' spaces like an afterthought. They decided to build a company that would do it differently.

Fourteen years later, SPOT serves over 400 commercial properties spanning corporate campuses, medical facilities, retail chains, and educational institutions. Our founding principle hasn't changed: every facility deserves cleaning professionals who care as much as you do.

We're not a franchise. We're not a staffing agency. Every SPOT technician is a direct employee — background-checked, trained, uniformed, and proud of the work they deliver.
