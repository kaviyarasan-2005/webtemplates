FAQ

Questions? Pop a Bubble

Click any question bubble to reveal the answer. Each question pops open with an answer inside.

How often should we schedule office cleaning?

Most offices with 20+ staff benefit from 3–5x weekly service. We'll recommend a frequency after a quick walkthrough of your space.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes — all professional-grade cleaning products and commercial equipment are provided by SPOT. You don't need to stock a single thing.

Can we use our preferred cleaning products?

Absolutely. If your facility requires specific EPA-listed disinfectants or fragrance-free products, we accommodate that at no extra charge.

What if we're not satisfied with a clean?

Call us within 24 hours and we return to re-clean the area in question — at zero additional cost. Our guarantee is unconditional.

Are the same cleaners sent each visit?

We assign a consistent crew to your account wherever possible. You'll recognize faces and they'll know your facility — that's by design.

How quickly can you start service?

Standard onboarding takes 3–5 business days after contract signing. Expedited starts within 48 hours are possible for urgent needs.
