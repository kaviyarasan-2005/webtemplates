FAQs

Common Questions

When does commission apply?

Commission is applied only to bookings, campaigns, and deals actively sourced and managed by your MUSE agent. You never pay commission on work you independently secure outside of MUSE representation scope.

Are there any upfront fees?

No upfront retainers for talent joining the roster. The only fees are commissions on earnings. Portfolio development may have optional production costs that are fully disclosed and agreed in advance.

How is commission calculated?

Commission is calculated on gross booking value before any taxes. An itemized statement is sent monthly showing every booking, deduction, and disbursement. Zero ambiguity.

What if I want to leave MUSE?

Our agreements run annually and either party may give 30 days written notice before renewal. Post-termination, MUSE retains commission on any deals in progress that were negotiated before termination.
