Our Story

A Story Worth Reading

INT. DOWNTOWN MANHATTAN LOFT — 2016
A drafty loft. Three desks pushed together. Two agents — former magazine editors — and one shared conviction:
ELEANOR VOSS
"Every extraordinary talent deserves an extraordinary advocate."
MUSE was incorporated on a Tuesday. By Thursday, they had their first client. By the end of 2016, they had twelve. By 2020 — a roster of over 200. Today, MUSE represents talent in 40 countries, manages campaigns worth hundreds of millions, and has placed talent in four Oscar-winning productions.
INT. MUSE HQ, NEW YORK — PRESENT DAY
The loft is now five floors in a West Village building. The conviction hasn't changed a single word.
