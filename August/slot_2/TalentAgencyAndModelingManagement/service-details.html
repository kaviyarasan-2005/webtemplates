Director's Notes

Questions Answered

Our agents annotate the most frequently asked questions — straight from the margin notes.

// Director's Note — Q1: How long does representation last?
Our standard agreement is one year, automatically renewable. Either party may give 30 days notice before renewal to terminate. We believe in earning your continued trust every single year.
// Director's Note — Q2: What is MUSE's commission rate?
Standard commission is 15% of gross earnings from bookings we source. Deals you bring yourself carry a reduced rate. Full transparency on all deductions in your monthly statement.
// Director's Note — Q3: How quickly will I start getting bookings?
Most new talents see their first booking within 60–90 days of signing. Portfolio development typically takes 2–4 weeks, then active submissions begin immediately.
// Director's Note — Q4: Can I continue with personal projects?
Absolutely. Your personal brand is yours. We only ask for right of first refusal on commercial partnerships where we have established client relationships.
