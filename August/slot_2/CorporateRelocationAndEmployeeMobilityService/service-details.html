Live Desk Chat Q&A

Common questions answered directly by senior relocation coordinators at our central London desk.

How long in advance should an enterprise book an executive move?
We recommend initiating the technical survey 4 to 6 weeks prior to target departure. However, for urgent corporate transfers, our expedited sprint team can complete packing and customs lodgment within 72 hours.
How are high-value artworks, wine collections, or pianos insured?
Specialized items receive bespoke custom skeletal timber crating and individual high-value item riders under our Lloyd's master policy, providing full replacement valuation with zero deductible.
Who oversees customs clearance in stringent jurisdictions like Singapore or Dubai?
MOVE employs in-house licensed customs brokers in every major hub. We pre-screen employee shipping declarations and duty exemption forms to prevent any port demurrage.
