Our Genesis

Architecting Precision Mobility

MOVE was established in 2009 by former global logistics executives and enterprise HR directors who recognized a glaring void: corporate mobility was treated as freight forwarding rather than an employee experience pillar.

Over the last seventeen years, we built a proprietary network spanning 120 countries, owning our transit hubs, customs brokers, and settling-in caseworkers. This unified infrastructure ensures zero third-party handoffs and 100% policy compliance.
