Pricing FAQs

Are customs duties included in the quote? +

No, standard quotes cover freight and handling. Duties and taxes are billed at cost based on destination country tariffs unless you specifically request a DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) service.

How do market fluctuations affect my rate? +

Spot rates are valid for 14 days. For contract clients, we peg rates to major freight indexes (like SCFI) with a fixed markup, ensuring transparency.

Do you offer trade finance? +

Yes, for qualified enterprise clients, we offer 30-90 day LC (Letter of Credit) terms to help manage cash flow during transit.
