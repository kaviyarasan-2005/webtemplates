Our Process

Your Aisle to a Perfect Day

Eight confident steps on the path from engagement to celebration — all guided by your personal planner.

01

Initial Enquiry

You share your story, date, and vision in a brief questionnaire. We respond within 48 hours.

02

Discovery Call

A 60-minute conversation with your planner to explore your vision, budget, and priorities in detail.

03

Proposal

We present a bespoke service proposal with timeline, investment, and our vision for your day.

04

Design Concept

Your personalised mood board, palette, and design direction are created and refined together.

05

Venue & Vendors

We source, visit, and secure your venue and vendor team — handling all negotiations on your behalf.

06

Planning Phase

Timelines, floor plans, seating charts, menus, and logistics — every detail documented and confirmed.

07

Rehearsal

A final walk-through of the ceremony and reception sequence with the wedding party and key vendors.

08

Your Wedding Day

We arrive early, oversee everything, and send you off into your happily ever after with a smile.
