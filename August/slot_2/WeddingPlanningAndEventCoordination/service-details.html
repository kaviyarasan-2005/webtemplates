Dear VOWS

Questions from Our Couples

An advice column in the spirit of our letters to couples — honest answers to the questions we hear most.

"When should we start planning our wedding?"

For a VOWS full planning service, we recommend reaching out 12–18 months before your intended date — and even earlier for peak-season or destination weddings. However, we've worked wonders with 6-month timelines. The earlier we start, the wider your options.

— Isabelle, Founder

"Do we have to use your recommended vendors?"

Not at all. While we have trusted relationships with an incredible network of vendors, you're always free to bring your own. We'll vet any external vendors, review contracts, and integrate them seamlessly into your planning. Your day, your team.

— Priya, Senior Planner

"How do you handle unexpected issues on the day?"

Every VOWS wedding has a comprehensive contingency plan — from weather alternatives to vendor no-shows. Our team drills every scenario in advance. On the day, problems are resolved before you ever know they exist. You celebrate; we handle everything else.

— Amara, Coordination Specialist

"What's included in the Full Planning fee?"

Unlimited planning meetings, full design direction and mood boarding, vendor sourcing and negotiation for 12+ categories, budget management, guest management, RSVPs, rehearsal coordination, and full day-of coordination for the lead planner plus an assistant. Vendor costs are quoted separately.

— Oliver, Design Director
