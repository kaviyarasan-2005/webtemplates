Our Story

Born from a Love of Love Itself

VOWS began in 2004 when founder Isabelle Laurent planned her sister's wedding and discovered her true calling: transforming love stories into unforgettable celebrations. What started as a one-woman passion project has grown into an award-winning agency of twelve dedicated planners.

Today, VOWS is recognised as one of the UK's premier wedding planning agencies, with destinations spanning five continents and a portfolio of over 500 ceremonies — from intimate garden vows to grand château celebrations.

Our philosophy is simple: no two love stories are the same, and no two weddings should be either. We listen first, plan second, and execute with obsessive attention to detail.

Our Journey

Blooms Along the Way

Two decades of growth, love, and milestones — each one a flower on our garland.

2004

Founded

Isabelle Laurent launches VOWS from a small London studio, planning her first three weddings by word of mouth.

2009

First Destination

VOWS plans its first international wedding — a cliff-edge ceremony in Positano, Italy, for 40 guests.

2016

500th Wedding

We celebrate a major milestone — our 500th wedding, a grand ballroom celebration for 350 guests in Edinburgh.

2020

National Award

VOWS wins the UK Wedding Awards' "Planning Agency of the Year" for the first time — and has held it every year since.

2024

20-Year Anniversary

Two decades of I Do's. We celebrate with a charity gala and commit to planting a tree for every wedding planned.

Our Vows to You

What We Promise

"We, VOWS, take you — to love, honour, and meticulously plan…"

We promise to listen first — your vision takes precedence over any trend or template. Every detail we suggest is rooted in who you are.

We promise to obsess over every detail — from the wording on your escort cards to the direction of your lighting rig, nothing is too small to matter.

We promise to stay calm on your day — so that you never have to. Every contingency is planned for, every vendor briefed, every moment mapped.

We promise total transparency — no hidden costs, no surprise invoices, no ambiguity. Just honest, clear partnership from first call to final farewell.
