Who We Are

From a Two-Person Shop to a Regional Authority

Tintex started in 2011 with one bay, one plotter, and a stubborn belief: automotive glass work should be held to the same standard as the manufacturers who built the car.

Today our Motor City studio runs four climate-controlled bays, a fleet of mobile service vans, and an in-house ADAS calibration lab. But the standard hasn't moved. Every panel still gets computer-cut, edge-shaved, and point-by-point inspected before it leaves the bay.

Never the Fastest — Always the Best

We schedule fewer jobs per day than we could take. A flawless install that takes four hours beats a rushed one that bubbles in a month.

Radical Transparency

You get the exact film spec, the exact price, and the exact reason a job takes what it takes. No vague quotes, no surprise line items, ever.

Certified, Not Self-Taught

Every technician on our floor holds current manufacturer certifications for the film brands we install and completes ADAS calibration training biannually.

What Drives Us

Four Rules We Never Break

These aren't slogans on the wall. They're the standards every job is measured against before the keys are handed back.

01

Precision Beats Speed

No blade ever touches your glass. We plot every film pattern digitally to the millimeter for your exact make and model, then micro-shave the edge for a seamless, borderless factory finish.

02

Warranty We Stand Behind

A lifetime warranty isn't a marketing line for us — it's a promise we can honor because we install product we trust. Bubbling, peeling, purpling, or a leak: we fix it free, no questions asked.

03

Documentation, Not Guesswork

Every install ships with a digital certificate of compliance, your VLT readings, and the calibration report for any ADAS system we serviced. Paperwork you can actually use.

04

The Customer Is Not a Queue

Fiber Wi-Fi, work desks, charging stations, and a 24/7 key drop box. You should be comfortable while we do meticulous work on your vehicle.

Milestones

Fifteen Years of Building

Every expansion was earned — funded by word-of-mouth from drivers who noticed the difference between a clean install and a careless one.

2011
2011

The First Bay Opens

Marcus Hale and Dan Reyes convert a two-bay auto detail shop in Motor City into a dedicated tint studio. One plotter, one heat gun, and a rule that no job leaves unfinished.

2014
2014

Manufacturer Certification Program

Tintex becomes an authorized installer for 3M, XPEL, and LLumar, and every technician completes formal factory training. The shift from "very good" to "certified" begins here.

2018
2018

In-House ADAS Calibration Lab

As camera-equipped windshields become standard, we build a dedicated calibration bay with laser-aligned static targets and dynamic road-scan verification — so safety systems are restored before the vehicle leaves.

2021
2021

Mobile Service Fleet Launches

Three fully equipped vans bring windshield replacement, chip repair, and calibration to driveways and office lots across the greater metro area — because some customers can't take a day off work.

2024
2024

Nano-Ceramic IR Film Line

We add Elite IR Matrix technology to the lineup — 98% infrared heat rejection with zero signal interference — and publish our full performance matrix so customers can compare grades side by side.
