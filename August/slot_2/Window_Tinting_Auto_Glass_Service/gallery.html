State Tint Laws & Legal Compliance

Understanding legal Visible Light Transmission (VLT) limits. Every installation at Tintex is certified 100% compliant with local and state motor vehicle laws.

Windshield Glass

Top 4–6" / 70% Clear

Non-reflective tint allowed along the top AS-1 manufacturer line. 70%+ Clear Nano-Ceramic IR film is permitted full-glass in most jurisdictions.

Front Side Windows

25% – 35% VLT

Must allow between 25% and 35% visible light transmission depending on specific state regulations for driver & passenger safety.

Rear Side Windows

Any Darkness (5%+)

SUVs, trucks, and vans are legally permitted any level of darkness on rear passenger windows, including dark 5% limo privacy film.

Rear Windshield

Any Darkness (5%+)

Permitted at any shade provided the vehicle has functional dual side exterior rearview mirrors on both driver and passenger doors.
