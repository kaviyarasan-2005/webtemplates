Our Services

Uncompromising quality in every installation. From premium ceramic window tint to precise auto glass replacement, we deliver perfection you can see.

Signature Service

Premium Window Tinting

We don't use standard dyed films that turn purple and bubble. Our advanced carbon and nano-ceramic tints provide the ultimate combination of aesthetics, privacy, and climate control.

  • Computer-Cut Precision: Every piece of film is precisely cut by software to perfectly match your vehicle's make and model, ensuring a flawless edge without taking a blade to your glass.
  • Advanced Heat Rejection: Our ceramic options block up to 60% of total solar heat energy and 99.9% of UV rays.
  • Shaved Edges: We meticulously shave the top edge of roll-down windows for a seamless, factory-finish look.

Safety Critical

Auto Glass Replacement

Your windshield provides up to 30% of your vehicle's structural strength in a rollover. We treat auto glass replacement with the strict safety standards it demands, using only premium materials.

  • OEM & OEE Glass: We install Original Equipment Manufacturer or high-quality Equivalent glass to ensure perfect fitment and optical clarity.
  • Advanced Adhesives: We use industry-leading urethane that cures quickly, allowing a safe drive-away time of just 1 hour.
  • ADAS Calibration: Integrated cameras for lane assist and emergency braking are fully recalibrated on-site after replacement.

Quick Fix

Rock Chip Repair

Don't let a small chip turn into an expensive crack. Our professional resin injection process stops damage from spreading, restores structural integrity, and significantly improves the cosmetic appearance of the damage.

  • Fast Service: Repairs take approximately 30 minutes. Walk-ins are generally welcome for this service.
  • Insurance Approved: Many comprehensive insurance policies cover chip repairs with $0 out-of-pocket cost to you.

Restoration

Professional Tint Removal

Is your old tint bubbling, peeling, or turning purple? Trying to peel it off yourself often leaves a stubborn, sticky adhesive residue and can permanently damage your rear defroster lines.

  • Safe Steaming: We use professional steamers to loosen the adhesive without harsh chemicals.
  • Defroster Protection: We meticulously remove film from the rear window to preserve your heating elements.
  • Perfect Prep: We leave the glass perfectly clean, ready for a fresh installation of our premium films.
