Signature Service

Premium Window Tinting

We don't use standard dyed films that turn purple and bubble. Our advanced carbon and nano-ceramic tints provide the ultimate combination of aesthetics, privacy, and climate control.

  • Computer-Cut Precision: Every piece of film is precisely cut by software to perfectly match your vehicle's make and model, ensuring a flawless edge without taking a blade to your glass.

  • Shaved Edges: We meticulously shave the top edge of roll-down windows for a seamless, factory-finish look.

Auto Glass Replacement

Your windshield provides up to 30% of your vehicle's structural strength in a rollover. We treat auto glass replacement with the strict safety standards it demands, using only premium materials.

  • OEM & OEE Glass: We install Original Equipment Manufacturer or high-quality Equivalent glass to ensure perfect fitment and optical clarity.
  • Advanced Adhesives: We use industry-leading urethane that cures quickly, allowing a safe drive-away time of just 1 hour.

Master Craftsmanship

Our 5-Stage Installation Workflow

Every vehicle goes through our multi-point clean-room process to guarantee a razor-sharp, bubble-free factory finish.

01

Multi-Stage Glass Decontamination

Clay bar and medical-grade slip prep remove embedded grime, micro-debris, and factory residues for a sterile surface.

02

Precision Computer Plotting

Digital templates cut film to the exact millimeter of your vehicle's glass specs. Zero blades are ever placed near your glass or trim.

03

Dry Heat Forming

Film is heat-molded to match the compound curvature of your vehicle's curved glass before application, eliminating tension.

04

Clean Bay Bonding

Installed inside our filtered, climate-controlled clean installation bay to prevent dust nibs and airborne contamination.

05

Edge Micro-Shaving & QC

Top edges are micro-shaved flush with the glass edge for a borderless OEM factory look, followed by a 12-point inspection.

Certified Safety Technology

On-Site ADAS Camera & Sensor Calibration

Modern windshields are equipped with critical Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) cameras for Lane Keep Assist, Automatic Emergency Braking, and Adaptive Cruise Control. Whenever glass is replaced, camera angles change and must be recalibrated to OEM safety tolerances.

Static Target Board Calibration

Laser-aligned target boards calibrate camera optical pitch and yaw inside our bay.

Dynamic Road Scan Testing

Live driving cycle verification ensures radar and camera locks track lane markings perfectly.

OEM Diagnostic Certificate

You receive a digital calibration certificate verified against vehicle factory specifications.

Insurance Covered
