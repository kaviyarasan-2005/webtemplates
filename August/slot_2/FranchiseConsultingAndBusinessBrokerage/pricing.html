Common Fee Questions

What is the Lehman Formula?
A sliding scale for M&A fees (e.g., 5% of the first million, 4% of the second, etc.). We use a modernized version for larger deals to ensure fair pricing.
Are valuation fees credited?
Yes, if you hire us to sell your business within 6 months of a paid valuation, the fee is credited toward the success fee at closing.
Who pays for franchise consulting?
Franchisors pay our placement fee. Our consulting services are 100% free for franchise buyers.
