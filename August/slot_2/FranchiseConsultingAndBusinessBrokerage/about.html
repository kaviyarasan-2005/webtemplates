Our Story

Integrity in Every Transaction

DEAL was founded on a simple premise: business brokerage needed more transparency and less friction. What started as a small boutique firm handling local main street businesses has grown into a national advisory network.

We understand that selling a business is often the culmination of a life's work, and buying one is the beginning of a new legacy. Our brokers are former founders, financial analysts, and franchise veterans who treat your capital as their own.
