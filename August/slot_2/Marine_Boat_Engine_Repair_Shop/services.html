Seasonal Service

Engine Winterization

Protect your engine from freeze damage, corrosion, and fuel degradation during the off-season. Our comprehensive winterization program has prevented thousands of dollars in spring repair costs for our customers.

Our Winterization Checklist:

Engine oil and filter change
Cooling system flush and antifreeze
Fuel stabilizer treatment
Fogging spray for cylinders
Battery disconnect and trickle charge
Lower unit gear oil change
Spark plug inspection
Corrosion protection application
