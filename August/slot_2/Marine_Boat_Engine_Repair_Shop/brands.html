Specialized Brand Services

What makes our service different for each specific manufacturer.

Mercury & MerCruiser

As a Mercury Authorized Service Center, we have full access to the proprietary Computer Diagnostic System (CDS) and G3 software. This allows us to rapidly diagnose SmartCraft systems, Verado superchargers, and OptiMax direct injection systems with factory-level precision.

Yamaha Outboards

Our Yamaha Master Technician leads a team fully equipped with YDIS (Yamaha Diagnostic Information System). We specialize in complex V6 and V8 SHO services, Command Link gauge integration, and Helm Master EX joystick control calibrations.

Honda & Suzuki

We stock a massive inventory of Honda and Suzuki OEM parts to ensure fast turnaround times on these reliable 4-strokes. From valve clearance adjustments to VVT (Variable Valve Timing) diagnostics, our techs know these engines inside and out.

Legacy Brands (Evinrude/Johnson)

While no longer in production, Evinrude E-TEC and older Johnson 2-strokes still power thousands of boats. Our senior technicians have the rare EV-Diagnostics software, specialized tools, and knowledge to keep these legacy engines running strong.

OEM vs. Aftermarket Parts

At Helm Marine, we strongly believe that using Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts is crucial for the longevity and performance of your marine engine.

Why we prefer OEM Parts
OEM parts are built to the exact specifications of the original engine designers. They ensure perfect fitment, maintain your factory warranty, and undergo rigorous marine-environment testing that aftermarket parts often skip.
When we use Aftermarket
We only use aftermarket parts in three scenarios: 1) The OEM part is obsolete/discontinued (common for engines 20+ years old), 2) A trusted aftermarket brand (like Sierra) has demonstrably improved upon a flawed OEM design, or 3) The customer explicitly requests it for budget reasons (warranty limitations apply).

Continuous Factory Training

Marine engine technology evolves rapidly. That's why our technicians spend an average of 40 hours per year in factory training centers, mastering the latest fuel injection systems, digital throttle and shift (DTS) technologies, and emissions controls.

Common Brand-Specific Services

We handle the unique maintenance requirements for each manufacturer.

Mercury Verado Services
Verado engines require specialized care including supercharger belt inspection, power steering system bleeding (for L6 models), pencil coil testing, and specific Mercury Synthetic Blend oil formulations. We carry all specialized Verado tools.
Yamaha VST System Cleaning
A common maintenance item on fuel-injected Yamahas is the Vapor Separator Tank (VST). We perform complete VST teardowns, ultrasonic cleaning of the high-pressure pump screen, and filter replacement to cure hesitation and stalling issues.
Honda Valve Clearances
Honda outboards utilize a mechanical valvetrain that requires periodic clearance checks and adjustments (typically every 400 hours). Our techs use precise feeler gauges to ensure your Honda runs quietly and maintains peak compression.
Suzuki Anode Replacements
Suzuki engines utilize several internal water jacket anodes to prevent galvanic corrosion. We perform complete internal and external anode inspections and replacements during annual services to protect your engine block.
