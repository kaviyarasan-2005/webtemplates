Our Journey Through the Years

From a small dockside garage to a full-service marine repair facility, AnchorPoint Marine has grown alongside the boating community we serve.

Est. 1998

Built on the Docks, Built to Last

AnchorPoint Marine was founded by master marine mechanic Robert "Captain Rob" Harland in 1998 with a simple mission: provide honest, expert marine engine repair at fair prices. Starting with just a toolbox and a rented bay at the marina, Captain Rob built a reputation for quality work and integrity that still drives everything we do today.

Today, our 12,000 sq. ft. facility serves over 2,000 boats annually, with a team of 14 factory-certified technicians specializing in all major marine engine brands.

1998

The Beginning

Captain Rob opens AnchorPoint Marine in a single-bay dockside garage at Marina Bay Harbor.

2004

First Expansion

Expanded to a 4-bay facility. Earned Mercury and Yamaha factory certifications.

2012

New Headquarters

Moved into our current 12,000 sq. ft. facility with state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment.

2024

Industry Leaders

Named Top Marine Service Center by Boating Magazine. Serving 40+ engine brands with 14 certified technicians.
