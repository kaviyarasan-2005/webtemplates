Our Location

We're conveniently located at Marina Bay Harbor with both deep water slips and easy trailer access.

Arriving by Trailer

Use the main Marina Bay entrance off Coastal Hwy. Proceed past the security gate and follow signs for Commercial Operations. We have a dedicated 50ft wide ramp for test runs and ample trailer turnaround space.

Arriving by Water

Navigate to Channel Marker 14 on the Intracoastal. Turn west into Marina Bay Harbor. We are located on Dock C, Slips 1-8. Hailing frequency is VHF Channel 16 or 68. Deep water access (12ft draft MLW).

Parking & Storage

Free customer parking is available in front of the main office. If leaving your boat for extended service, it will be securely stored in our fenced, video-monitored dry lot or inside our climate-controlled bay.
