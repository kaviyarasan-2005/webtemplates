Pricing & Payment FAQs

Common questions about our billing process, estimates, and payment terms.

Do you charge for diagnostics?
Yes, our standard diagnostic fee is $125. This covers up to one hour of a certified technician's time utilizing our specialized computer diagnostic equipment. However, if you authorize us to perform the recommended repairs based on the diagnostic results, we waive this fee entirely.
Will the final bill exceed the estimate?
We strictly adhere to a "no surprises" policy. If our technicians discover additional issues once repairs have begun that would cause the final bill to exceed the original estimate by more than 10%, we will halt work and contact you for authorization before proceeding.
Do I need to pay a deposit?
For standard maintenance (like winterization or oil changes), no deposit is required. For major repairs or jobs requiring special order parts that total over $1,000, we require a 50% deposit upfront, with the remainder due upon completion and your satisfaction.
Can I supply my own parts?
To guarantee our work and maintain our quality standards, we prefer to supply all parts used in our repairs. If you insist on providing your own parts, we charge a higher labor rate ($165/hour) and cannot provide any warranty on the repair or the labor involved.

Every boat is different. If you don't see your specific service listed, reach out to us with your engine details and symptoms for a personalized estimate.
