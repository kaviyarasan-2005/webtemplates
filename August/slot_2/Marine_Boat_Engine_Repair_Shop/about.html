Est. 1998

Built on the Docks, Built to Last

Helm Marine began in 1998 when master marine mechanic Robert "Captain Rob" Harland rented a single bay at Marina Bay Harbor. Armed with a toolbox, a factory service manual, and a stubborn belief that boaters deserved honest answers, he quickly earned a reputation for repairs that lasted.

Twenty-five years later, that same philosophy runs through a 12,000 sq. ft. facility, a team of 14 factory-certified technicians, and more than 12,500 engines brought back to life. We still answer the phone ourselves, still write every estimate by hand, and still treat each boat as if it were headed to our own dock.

We are not a chain. We are the shop at the end of the marina — the one your neighbor recommends.

What Drives Us

Three principles we refuse to compromise on, whether it's a routine oil change or a full engine rebuild.

Precision Craftsmanship

Every repair is performed to manufacturer torque specifications and tolerances, then verified with computer diagnostics and a dock test before your boat leaves the yard.

Honest, Upfront Pricing

You receive a detailed written estimate before we touch a bolt. If we find something unexpected, we call first — no surprise line items, no pressure, no hidden fees.

Boaters First

Short seasons and long weekends matter. We prioritize emergency repairs, answer questions in plain language, and stand behind our work long after you've left the dock.

Milestones That Shaped Us

From a single rented bay to one of the coast's most trusted marine service centers.
