Our Story

15 Years of Sonic Excellence

Founded in 2010 by veteran audio engineer Marcus Webb, SoundForge Studios was built on a singular vision — create a space where artistic freedom meets technical perfection. What started as a single recording room has grown into a multi-suite facility housing some of the world's finest audio equipment.

From indie artists tracking their debut albums to platinum-selling musicians crafting chart-toppers, every project receives the same unwavering dedication to sonic excellence that has defined our reputation for over a decade.
