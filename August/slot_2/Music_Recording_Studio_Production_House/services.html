What We Do

Audio Production Services

From capturing the raw emotion of a vocal take to the final limiting on a master bus, we provide end-to-end audio production services tailored to your sonic vision.

Recording & Tracking

Capture performances with unparalleled clarity in our acoustically perfected live rooms and isolation booths. Our microphone locker features both vintage classics and modern precision tools.

  • Vocal Tracking: Floating-floor isolation booths with zero reflection points.
  • Live Band Recording: Large tracking room accommodating up to 12 musicians simultaneously.
  • Drum Tracking: Specialized drum room with adjustable acoustics and 16-channel dedicated inputs.
  • Voiceover & ADR: Broadcast-quality recording for film, TV, and commercial applications.
Room A & B
SSL 9000 J

Professional Mixing

Transform raw multitracks into a cohesive, impactful mix. We blend the warmth of analog outboard gear with the precision of digital processing to create mixes that translate perfectly to any system.

  • Analog Summing: Mixed through our SSL 9000 J-Series console for unmatched depth and width.
  • Vocal Tuning & Editing: Transparent pitch correction (Melodyne/Auto-Tune) and precise rhythmic alignment.
  • Stem Mixing: Delivery of isolated stems (vocals, drums, bass, synths) for live performance or remixing.
  • Dolby Atmos Mixing: Immersive spatial audio mixes compatible with Apple Music and Tidal.

Audio Mastering

The final polish that prepares your music for commercial release. We ensure competitive loudness, dynamic balance, and tonal consistency across your entire project.

  • Digital & Streaming: Optimized targeting for Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube loudness standards (-14 LUFS).
  • Vinyl Pre-Mastering: Specialized mastering preserving dynamics and addressing RIAA equalization requirements.
  • Album Sequencing: Custom fades, gaps, and metadata encoding (ISRC, CD-Text) for DDP image creation.
  • Audio Restoration: Noise reduction, click removal, and rescue of poorly recorded audio.

Full Production

Bring us a demo, a lyric, or just a concept. Our in-house producers will work with you to build the track from the ground up, providing instrumentation, arrangement, and creative direction.

  • Beatmaking & Programming: Custom drum programming, synthesis, and sampling tailored to your genre.
  • Session Musicians: Access to our network of elite studio musicians (guitarists, string quartets, horns).
  • Vocal Coaching: In-session guidance to help you deliver your best, most emotionally resonant performance.
  • Songwriting Polish: Structural arrangement and melodic refinement to maximize commercial potential.
