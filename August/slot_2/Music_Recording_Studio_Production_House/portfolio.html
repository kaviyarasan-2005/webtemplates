The SoundForge Release Pipeline

How every portfolio release journeys from raw multi-track tracking to audiophile master distribution.

01

Pre-Production & Reference Analysis

Acoustic room alignment, tempo mapping, phase correlation audits, and high-headroom tracking at 96kHz / 32-bit float.

  • Reference spectrum comparison
  • Mic placement calibration
  • Headroom staging
02

Hybrid Analog Mixing

48-channel SSL console summing combined with hardware tube compressors, Pultec equalizers, and harmonic tape saturation.

  • SSL 9000 J analog summing
  • Outboard surgical dynamic control
  • Transparent vocal tuning
03

Dolby Atmos Spatialization

Native 3D object rendering in a 7.1.4 certified environment, calibrated for Apple Music Spatial and Tidal Hi-Fi playback.

  • 7.1.4 Speaker calibration
  • Binaural headphone tuning
  • Immersive stem positioning
04

Master Delivery & Archival

Apple Digital Master certification, DMM vinyl pre-cut masters, -14 LUFS DSP streaming versions, and complete stem archiving.

  • Apple Digital Masters badge
  • DMM Vinyl cut ready pre-masters
  • Lossless 24-bit 96kHz packaging
