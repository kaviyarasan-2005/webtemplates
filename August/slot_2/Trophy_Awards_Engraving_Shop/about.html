Our Story

Crafting Excellence Since 1998

For over two decades, CREST has been the trusted name in premium trophies, plaques, medals, and custom engravings — turning achievements into timeless keepsakes.

Who We Are

A Legacy Built on Precision

Founded in 1998 by master engraver Robert Goldwell, CREST began as a small workshop with a single laser engraver and a passion for perfection. Today, we operate multiple studios with a team of skilled artisans, combining traditional craftsmanship with cutting-edge laser, rotary, and UV printing technology.

Every award we create tells a story — from school sports days and graduation ceremonies to corporate galas and championship events. Our mission is simple: deliver uncompromising quality, attention to detail, and service that exceeds expectations.
