Engraving Methods

Choose Your Engraving Style

Each technique produces a distinct aesthetic. Our team recommends the best method for your specific material and design.

Most Popular

CO2 & Fiber Laser Engraving

Our flagship technique uses high-powered CO2 and fiber lasers for sub-millimeter precision. Ideal for logos, photographs, fine text, and complex artwork on crystal, glass, wood, and acrylic.

Photographic detail possible
Works on crystal, glass, wood, acrylic, leather
3D subsurface engraving in crystal
Fast turnaround for large quantities
Most Durable

Rotary Spindle Engraving

A spinning cutting tool physically removes material, creating deep, tactile channels in the surface. The result is permanent, high-contrast engraving that cannot be rubbed or worn away.

Deep physical cut — permanent result
Ideal for metal, brass, aluminum plaques
Tactile texture adds premium feel
Can be filled with contrasting color paint
Most Vibrant

UV Flatbed Color Printing

UV-cured inks are applied directly to the surface, producing photographic full-color results. Combined with laser engraving for text, this creates stunning mixed-media awards.

Full CMYK photographic color
White underbase for dark materials
Scratch and UV-resistant finish
Can combine with laser engraving
Most Elegant

Diamond-Drag Scribing

A diamond-tipped stylus is dragged across polished metal surfaces, creating bright, reflective lines. Used for fine jewellery-style text on high-end trophies and premium plaques.

Bright reflective cut — jewellery quality
Best on polished metals and coated surfaces
Italic script fonts look exceptional
Premium finish for executive awards
