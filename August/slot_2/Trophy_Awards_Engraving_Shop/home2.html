Premium Materials

The Materials of Excellence

Each material brings its own story. We select only the finest to ensure your award endures.

Optical Crystal Awards

Hand-crafted from 100% optical crystal — the material of choice for the most prestigious awards. Crystal's clarity and weight communicate excellence without words.

Optical Clarity
Museum-grade clarity with zero distortion for perfect laser engraving results.
Substantial Weight
Premium density gives crystal awards a gravitas that plastic alternatives cannot match.
Lifetime Durability
Crystal does not yellow, fade, or deteriorate — your award looks new for generations.

Metal & Brass Awards

Brass, aluminum, and zinc alloy — metal awards convey authority and tradition. Ideal for plaques, medals, and corporate recognition pieces that command respect.

Multiple Finishes
Antique bronze, brushed gold, silver chrome, or custom powder coating available.
Deep Engraving
Rotary engraving cuts deep for crisp, tactile text that cannot be rubbed away.

Natural Wood Awards

Rosewood, walnut, and cherry wood bring warmth and natural beauty to awards. Each piece has unique grain patterns — no two are exactly alike.

Sustainably Sourced
All wood materials are ethically and sustainably sourced from certified forests.
Laser Burning
Precision laser engraving creates rich contrast in the natural wood grain.

Acrylic Awards

Modern, vibrant, and versatile — acrylic awards allow for full-color printing and creative shapes impossible in other materials. Perfect for creative industries.

Full Color Printing
UV-cured inks produce photographic-quality full-color results on acrylic surfaces.
Custom Shapes
CNC cutting allows virtually any shape — logos, silhouettes, or abstract forms.
Our Craft

Three Techniques, One Standard of Excellence

We choose the right engraving technique for every material, ensuring lasting quality and perfect results.

01
Most Precise

Laser Engraving

Our state-of-the-art CO2 and fiber laser systems engrave with sub-millimeter accuracy. Ideal for crystal, glass, and acrylic — capturing logos, photographs, and intricate designs with perfect clarity.

02
Most Durable

Diamond-Drag Rotary

A diamond-tipped tool physically cuts into metal and brass. The result is deep, permanent lettering with a tactile quality that laser engraving cannot replicate — essential for fine metal plaques and trophies.

03
Most Vibrant

UV Color Printing

Ultraviolet-cured inks adhere directly to the award surface, enabling photo-quality full-color printing. Perfect for logo-heavy designs or when color impact is the priority.
