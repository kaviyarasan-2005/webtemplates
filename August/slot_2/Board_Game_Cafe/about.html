How Hexa Began

From Crowded Living Rooms to Downtown's Living Room

Hexa began around a cramped apartment coffee table in 2021. Our co-founders, Michael and Sarah, hosted weekly board game nights that quickly outgrew their living room. Between balancing drinks on precarious stacks of books, trying to decipher 30-page rulebooks after a long workday, and spilling soda on unsleeved cards, they realized what tabletop enthusiasts truly needed: a dedicated, welcoming home away from home.

In late 2023, Hexa opened its doors in the Downtown Arts District. We set out to eliminate every single friction point that stops people from playing: complicated rulebooks, bad lighting, cramped tables, and noisy environments.

  • Zero Table Turnover Rush: When you take a table at Hexa, it's yours. Savor a 4-hour heavy eurogame campaign without anyone hovering.
  • Resident Game Gurus on Demand: Don't read the manual. Our Gurus recommend titles, set up the boards, and teach you in five minutes.
  • Component-Safe Kitchen: Delicious flatbreads, paninis, and skewers specifically formulated to be finger-friendly and mess-free.

Crafted for Gamers

Built From the Ground Up for Tabletop

Every piece of furniture, lighting fixture, and architectural detail at Hexa was custom engineered to maximize gaming comfort.

Sunken Table Vaults

Solid Appalachian oak tables feature 4-inch deep sunken vaults with micro-suede playing mats. Dice never roll off the edge, and cards are effortless to pick up.

Recessed Cup Holders

Integrated below table edges so iced lattes, craft ales, and artisan teas stay completely separate from rare board components and character sheets.

Card-Friendly Lighting

Custom CRI-95+ warm overhead illumination engineered to eliminate glare on glossy cards, transparent card sleeves, and detailed miniature maps.

Acoustic Comfort

Sound-dampening acoustic felt baffles and cork wall paneling mean even during lively party game laughter, your table can easily hear strategy whispers.
