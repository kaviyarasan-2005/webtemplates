How A Visit Works

First time visiting Hexa? Here's how easy and welcoming your session will be.

1

Grab a Table

Walk in anytime or book ahead online. Pay a flat $5 cover charge per person for unlimited gaming all day — no ticking hourly timers.

2

Pick from 500+ Games

Browse our organized library shelves or ask a friendly Game Guru for a personalized recommendation tailored to your group size and taste.

3

Savor, Roll & Play

Order artisan espresso, craft cocktails, and component-safe paninis brought straight to your table while your Guru teaches the rules.

Component-Safe Dining

Kitchen & Bar

Fuel Your Tabletop Adventures

We believe great board games deserve exceptional food. Enjoy locally roasted single-origin espresso, themed craft cocktails, and oven-baked shareable plates brought right to your gaming table.

  • Component-Safe Finger Foods: Bavarian pretzel bites, warm paninis, and wood-fired flatbreads served with cocktail forks.
  • Specialty Espresso & Teas: Bottomless drip coffee, 24-hour nitro cold brew, and spiced cardamom chai lattes.
  • Themed Craft Cocktails & Brews: Color-shifting Empress gin elixirs, non-alcoholic potions, and rotating local craft IPAs.
