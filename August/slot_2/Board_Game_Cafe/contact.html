Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to play the games?

Not at all! Our Game Gurus wear brightly colored lanyards and are ready to teach you any game in our library.

Can I bring my own games?

Yes, absolutely! You can bring your own games from home. The standard cover charge still applies.

Is the cafe kid-friendly?

Yes, we have a dedicated section for family and kids games. We ask that children under 12 be accompanied by an adult.
