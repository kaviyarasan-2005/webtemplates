Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cafe covers, table rules, and gameplay atmosphere.

How does the table gaming cover charge work?

Our standard $5 table cover gives you unlimited access to play all 500+ board games in our library for as long as you'd like. Free for children under 5 and included with all Monthly Memberships.

Do I need to know the rules before playing?

Not at all! Our resident Game Gurus wear terracotta lanyards and are always ready to recommend titles, set up the boards, and teach your table the rules in 5 minutes.

Can I bring my own board games from home?

Yes, absolutely! You are welcome to bring your personal games, miniatures, or unboxed campaigns. The standard cover charge still applies for table seating and service.

Can I walk in or do I always need a reservation?

Walk-ins are always warmly welcomed! We reserve over 40% of our floor for drop-ins. However, for Friday nights, weekends, and groups of 5+, reserving online guarantees your table.

Are food and drinks allowed at the gaming tables?

Yes! Our kitchen specializes in table-safe, non-greasy shareable flatbreads and snacks. Every table is equipped with side cup holders and card-sleeve protectors to keep boards pristine.

Can I book private suites or host corporate events?

Yes! We offer two sound-insulated gaming suites accommodating 12 to 35 guests with private Gurus, tournament setups, and customized catering platters. Reach out via the form above!
