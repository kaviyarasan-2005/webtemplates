FAQ

INCOMING // CH-01
What is the maximum chargeable weight per shipment?

There is no hard maximum for air freight chargeable weight — we handle shipments from 1kg to full charter loads. For shipments over 1,000 kg chargeable, we automatically evaluate charter vs. allotment options and present the best rate.

— Bridge, Over.

INCOMING // CH-02
How do I track my airfreight shipment in real time?

All shipments booked through SHIP receive a tracking reference. Enter it on our Track page for live status, or log into your dashboard where push alerts fire at each milestone — departed, landed, in customs, delivered.

— Bridge, Over.

INCOMING // CH-03
Can SHIP handle dangerous goods on air freight?

Yes. Our air freight team includes IATA-certified DG specialists across all classes (1 through 9 where air-permissible). We prepare all DG documentation, shipper's declarations and airline notifications. Simply flag DG at booking stage.

— Bridge, Over.

INCOMING // CH-04
What is your fastest door-to-door transit time?

For next-flight-out express on major corridors (Singapore–London, Dubai–New York, Hong Kong–Frankfurt), we achieve 24–36 hour door-to-door. This includes same-day pickup, priority boarding, express customs clearance and courier delivery at destination.

— Bridge, Over.
