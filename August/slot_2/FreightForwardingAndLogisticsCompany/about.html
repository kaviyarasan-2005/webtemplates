About SHIP

The Crew Behind the Cargo

Sixteen years of global freight expertise, 180+ country network, and a team that treats every shipment as a mission.

Our Story

From Singapore to the World

Founded in 2010 by a team of customs brokers and logistics engineers, SHIP began as a boutique forwarder connecting South-East Asia to European industrial buyers. We built our technology stack before it was fashionable — because we knew that real-time visibility was not a feature, it was the product.

By 2018 we had offices on six continents. Today, SHIP manages over 140,000 shipments annually across air, sea, land and bonded warehouse networks — all orchestrated through a single operations platform.
