Safety First

Your Safety Is Our Priority

Before every session, our certified coaches walk all participants through a comprehensive safety briefing. All rules must be acknowledged before stepping to the throwing line.

Closed-Toe Shoes Required

All participants must wear closed-toe shoes at all times within the throwing area.

Eyes on the Lane

Keep your focus on your lane. Never walk forward to retrieve axes without coach clearance.

Throw One at a Time

Only one person throws at a time. All others remain behind the marked waiting line.

Alcohol Policy

Participants showing signs of intoxication will be removed from the throwing area. Safety first, always.

No Horseplay

Axes are sharp tools. Running, roughhousing, or misuse of equipment is strictly prohibited.

Report Incidents Immediately

Any injury or equipment concern must be reported to staff immediately. Our team is fully first-aid certified.

Getting Started

Axe Throwing 101

Never thrown an axe before? No problem. It takes about 3 minutes to get your first bullseye. Here's how it works:

1

Grip & Stance

Your coach demonstrates the two-handed grip and shows you the correct throwing stance — feet shoulder-width apart, dominant foot slightly back.

2

Wind-Up & Release

Bring the axe over your head with controlled motion. Release at the point where your arms are fully extended — let the axe rotate naturally.

3

Score & Improve

Watch the axe stick and score! Our digital tracking records every throw. Get real-time coaching tips and work toward that perfect bullseye.
