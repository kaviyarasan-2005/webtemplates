Quick Answers

Before You Contact Us

A few things our new clients commonly ask before reaching out.

Is the initial consultation genuinely free?

Yes, absolutely. There is no charge for visiting our showroom, discussing your project, or receiving a written quote. We believe in building trust before asking for a commitment.

How soon can you start my project?

Current lead time is 2–3 weeks for standard projects. Rush service is available for an additional charge. We will always confirm your start date before collecting your furniture.

Can I see a 3D preview of my furniture in the new fabric?

For projects above ₹25,000, we offer a complimentary digital fabric visualisation — your furniture rendered in your chosen fabric choices, so you can see the result before we begin. Ask about this during your consultation.
