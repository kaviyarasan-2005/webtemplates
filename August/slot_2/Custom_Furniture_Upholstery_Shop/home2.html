Our Heritage

A Quarter Century of
Dedicated Craftsmanship

Founded in 1998 by master upholsterer Krishnaswamy Pillai, ReVox began as a small workshop in Mylapore with a single sewing machine and an unwavering commitment to quality that has never wavered.

Today, our workshop spans 4,000 sq ft and houses 18 master artisans, each with specializations ranging from antique restoration to contemporary modular furniture. We have hand-stitched furniture for private residences, five-star hotels, film sets, and heritage buildings across South India.

Our philosophy is simple: every piece of furniture carries the memory and meaning of the people who own it. Our job is not just to upholster — it is to honour that story and write the next chapter.
