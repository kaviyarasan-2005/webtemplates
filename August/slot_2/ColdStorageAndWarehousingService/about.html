Our Story

From a Single Freezer to a Cold Chain Network

Founded in 2004 with a single 500-pallet cold room, COLD has grown into a multi-facility cold storage and warehousing network serving pharmaceutical, food, biotech, and specialty industries across the region.

Our commitment to temperature precision and product safety has earned the trust of over 200 clients, from small-batch artisan producers to multinational pharmaceutical companies.

Every decision we make is guided by a single principle: your products deserve the same care as if they were our own. That's why we invest relentlessly in monitoring technology, staff training, and facility upgrades.
