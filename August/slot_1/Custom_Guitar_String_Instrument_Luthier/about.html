The FRET Story

Dedicated to the art of handcrafted string instruments.

Our Story · Established 1988

From a Portland Garage Bench to World Concert Stages

The story of FRET began with a rebellion against the heavy finishes and automated shortcuts of mass production—rediscovering the soulful, unhindered resonance of pre-war acoustic lutherie.

When master luthier Arthur Pendelton set up his first bench in a damp Portland garage in 1988, modern guitar manufacturing was moving toward automation, synthetic lacquers, and overbuilt bracing. Arthur believed the soul of the instrument had been forgotten.

By painstakingly restoring pre-war Martins, Gibsons, and European parlor guitars, Arthur reverse-engineered the golden era of lutherie. He revived the exclusive use of organic hot hide glue—prized for its crystalline hardness that transfers vibrational acoustic energy with zero dampening between spruce top and scalloped brace.

As our small atelier expanded over three decades to include custom electrics and archtops, we established an immutable rule: no CNC machines will ever shape our necks or voice our soundboards. A machine can follow toolpaths, but only human fingertips can sense the wood grain tension, tap-tuned overtones, and tactile balance that transform timber into music.

"A machine mills timber to uniform dimensions. A master luthier carves tonewood to acoustic truth."

— Arthur Pendelton, Founder & Master Luthier
38 Years of Bench Provenance
Chronicles of the Atelier

Four Decades of Uncompromising Evolution

1988
The Garage Workbench

Arthur Pendelton opens the first one-room shop in Portland, focusing on pre-war acoustic restoration and developing our proprietary hand-tap voicing methods.

2002
The Hide Glue Revelation

Complete transition to traditional hot protein hide glue and hand-graduated Adirondack tops, earning international acclaim from the Guild of American Luthiers.

2014
Electric Atelier Founded

Master artisan Elena Rostova joins FRET to launch our electric division, pioneering hand-scatter-wound pickups and chambered resonant bodies with thin nitro finishes.

Today
Generational Heirlooms

Over 1,250 handcrafted instruments in the hands of touring artists, composers, and collectors across 42 nations—each backed by an archival lifetime warranty.

Guiding Ethos

Our Mission & Vision

Every shaving planed, glue seam joined, and string tuned in our workshop is governed by two timeless North Stars: an unwavering acoustic mission and an enduring generational vision.

Purpose & Craft

Our Mission

"To sculpt acoustic and electric instruments that serve as pure, unhindered extensions of the musician's creative voice."

We exist to safeguard the living tradition of hand-voiced lutherie against the tide of automated mass manufacturing. We believe that music made with deep intention requires an instrument with organic dynamic range, harmonic overtone bloom, and seamless ergonomic response. We achieve this by personally selecting vault-seasoned tonewoods, voicing every top by ear, and crafting each instrument directly for the artist.

✓
100% Hand-Voiced Soundboards: Every soundboard brace is hand-planed to harmonize with that specific timber's natural resonant nodes.
✓
Acoustic Transparency: Hand-rubbed 4-micron nitrocellulose finishes that allow tonewood cells to breathe, vibrate, and age gracefully.
✓
Uncompromised Integrity: Zero synthetic wood fillers, zero artificial wood baking, and zero automated neck carving shortcuts.
Legacy & Horizon

Our Vision

"To be the world's definitive benchmark atelier where generational craftsmanship and timeless tone endure for centuries."

We envision a musical world where string instruments are cherished as lifelong heirloom companions rather than disposable consumer items. By mentoring apprentices in classical woodworking disciplines and championing ethical forest stewardship, FRET aspires to cultivate an enduring legacy—building guitars that will sound richer, sweeter, and more resonant in fifty years than on the day they left our workbench.

✦
Apprentice Fellowship Program: Training the next generation of master luthiers in pre-war acoustic joinery and tap-tuning disciplines.
✦
Tonewood Conservation Initiative: Replanting ten native heritage hardwoods for every instrument built in our workshop.
✦
Centurial Longevity Guarantee: Building with reversible organic hide glue so every instrument can be maintained and restored for centuries.

The FRET Benchmark

Why Choose FRET

In an era dominated by assembly lines and commercial factory branding, discover the six pillars of master craft that make our workshop the choice of discerning musicians worldwide.

10–25 Yr Vault-Aged Tonewoods

We never use force-dried or kiln-baked lumber. All soundboards, backs, and sides are naturally seasoned in our climate vault for up to a quarter century, ensuring maximum cellular stability, pitch holding, and warm acoustic maturity from the first strum.

Appalachian Spruce · Brazilian Rosewood · Flamed Maple

100% Hand-Voiced Soundboards

Factory guitars stamp uniform brace shapes. Arthur individually tap-tunes and scallops each brace with micro-planes by ear, adjusting for the specific density and flex of each top billet to achieve harmonic overtone bloom and dynamic responsiveness.

Voiced by Ear · Scalloped Pre-War Bracing

Hot Hide Glue & 4-Micron Nitro

We join sound-critical components with organic hot hide glue that cures crystalline hard, transferring vibration with zero acoustic damping. Finished in ultra-thin nitrocellulose lacquer that lets tonewood breathe and age into a rich patina.

Crystalline Resonance · Fully Reversible

Bespoke Ergonomic Hand-Carves

Every neck profile is carved by hand to match your specific grip geometry, thumb position, and hand span. Jescar nickel or stainless frets are individually crowned and mirror-polished with rolled fingerboard edges for fatigue-free performance.

Tailored Neck Carve · Rolled Fret Edges

Direct Master Builder Collaboration

You collaborate directly with Arthur, Elena, or James—never sales middlemen. Throughout the creation of your instrument, you receive dedicated photo and video journal entries detailing timber selection, brace shaping, and final bench setups.

1-on-1 Consultations · Personal Build Journal

Archival Passport & Lifetime Care

Every FRET instrument arrives with a hand-bound leather build passport detailing timber harvest provenance, blueprint specs, and tap frequencies. Backed by our unconditional lifetime bench warranty with free seasonal adjustments and setups.
