Our Heritage

Where Time Meets Handcraft

Founded in 1988 in Portland, Oregon, FRET was born from a singular conviction: an instrument must feel like a natural anatomical extension of the player.

We don't use CNC profiling machines for our neck carves. We shape each neck with Japanese spoke shaves, checking the palm curve against hand anatomy, feeling wood grain under string tension, and adjusting relief by hand. No two trees grow identically, and no two instruments on our bench sound alike.
