Our Heritage

Where Time Meets Handcraft

Founded in 1988 in the Pacific Northwest, FRET was created to push the boundaries of acoustic physics without abandoning classical woodworking tradition.

We believe a truly great instrument cannot be churned out on high-speed industrial assembly lines. Wood is an organic material — no two spruce boards flex identically under tension. By hand-voicing every top and shaping every neck with traditional Japanese spoke shaves, we bring out the singular musical personality hidden inside each tree.

How It's Made

The Journey of a Build

Detailed Process
01

Tonewood Curation

Selecting resonant billets of old-growth spruce and rosewood, aged minimum 10 years in our moisture-controlled vault.

02

Tap-Tuning & Voicing

Graduating the soundboard with finger planes and scalloping tone bars until acoustic harmonic frequencies peak.

03

Hand Dovetail Joinery

Traditional hot hide glue and hand-carved dovetail neck joints ensuring direct acoustic coupling into the body.

04

Nitrocellulose Lacquer

18 micro-thin coats of traditional nitrocellulose lacquer, cured and hand-buffed for crystalline resonance.

05

Master Intonation

Fret dressing under string tension, bone nut carving, and microscopic saddle compensation for effortless action.
