The Bench Workflow

Every instrument undergoes our verified 4-stage surgical process from intake inspection to concert delivery.

1

Internal Inspection

Dental mirror and endoscopic camera inspection of soundboard braces, bridge plate wear, and neck angle geometry.

2

Hide Glue Stabilization

Re-cleating fractures and clamping loose tone bars with 145°F hot hide glue for 100% reversible structural integrity.

3

Surgical Fretwork

Precision fingerboard leveling, crowning wire with diamond abrasives, and hand-buffing fret crowns to mirror finish.

4

Voicing & Concert Setup

Settling under true string tension, strobe intonation tuning, action calibration, and comprehensive photographic report.

Repair & Restoration Questions

Hot protein hide glue dries glass-hard and crystal-clear with zero acoustic damping. Unlike modern PVA glues (Titebond) which exhibit plastic creep under string tension, hide glue transmits 100% of the string's mechanical vibrational energy into the soundboard. Furthermore, it is fully reversible with heat and moisture, allowing future generations of luthiers to service the instrument without tearing the wood fibers.
We re-hydrate the soundboard inside our 50% RH humidity vault to draw the crack edges naturally together. We then introduce hot hide glue with capillary suction, align the crack flush using specialized rare-earth magnetic clamping cauls, and cleat the underside with cross-grain Adirondack spruce diamonds. The repair is stabilized permanently with zero added weight.
