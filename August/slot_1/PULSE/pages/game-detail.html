Capture the Flag

Strike the enemy core, survive the alley, and bring it home.

Mission Brief

Objective

Two four-player squads spawn on opposite sides of the arena. A glowing core sits at each base. The goal is brutally simple: grab the enemy core and carry it back to your own base without being tagged out.

Carriers move slower, can't fire, and light up in team color. Death drops the core where you fell — everyone on the floor can hear the alert.

  • 3 cores on the map, respawn every 60s
  • Escorts screen the carrier through the alley
  • Stealing a captured core negates that score
  • First to 5 captures wins the round

Rules

Know the code before you hit the floor.

Team SizesStrict 4v4. Substitutions allowed between rounds only.
Time Limit15-minute rounds. Most captures at the buzzer wins.
ScoringTag = 1 point, steal = stop enemy score, capture = 3 points.
Respawn5-second respawn at your base. Carrier drop triggers a 20s core lock.
Restricted ZonesThe center alley is no-camper territory. Staying past 10s triggers elimination.
