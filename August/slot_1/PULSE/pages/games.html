Mission Briefing

Answers to the questions every new player asks.

Standard arena missions are open to ages 7+. Players under 7 can join the junior tactical course. Ages 12+ can enter the full arena unsupervised by an adult.
Standard lobbies hold up to 40 players. Private arena bookings take squads from 8 to 60 with custom match lengths and dedicated marshals.
Every player gets a sensor vest, glow band, and optional head protection. All emitters are IR-safe Class-1 eye-safe. Marshals are CPR certified and the floor is sanitized between sessions.
Standard matches run 10 to 20 minutes. Sessions include full gear-up, briefing, gameplay, and scoreboard review — plan about 45 minutes per booking.
Food and drink stay in the lounge and arcade areas. Energy stations are positioned outside the gate for hydration between rounds.
