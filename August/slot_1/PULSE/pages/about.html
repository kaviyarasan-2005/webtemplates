Origin File

Our Origin

PULSE started as a weekend side project between two former esports teammates who wanted a real-world arena that felt as sharp as a digital one. They bought a derelict distribution floor, welded the barriers themselves, and built the first sensor system in a garage.

The opening weekend sold out in three hours. The queue for the VR pods stretched into the parking lot. That proved the vision: people crave environments that turn play into spectacle.

Today the arena spans 28,000 square feet, but the ethos is unchanged. Every wall panel, blaster, and lighting rig is tuned by the same crew that swept the floors on day one.
