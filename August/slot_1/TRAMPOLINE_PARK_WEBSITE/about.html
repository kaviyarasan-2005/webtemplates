Our Origin

Where Gravity Becomes Optional

JUMP was founded in 2018 when two former competitive gymnasts realized their community lacked a safe, high-flying recreational haven for all generations.

What began in a modest 4-court warehouse has transformed into a high-energy arena featuring 12,000+ square feet of interconnected trampolines, foam pits, dodgeball courts, and ninja obstacles.

Over the past eight years, we've remained unwavering in our original mission: provide an unforgettable, inclusive space where kids gain confidence, athletes hone skills, and parents make memories side by side with their children.

Community Commitment

Every Jump Can Make a Difference

We believe local businesses should lift their hometowns higher. JUMP is dedicated to creating accessible recreation and giving back to Jumpville's youth and educational programs.

  • School Fundraiser Nights: 25% of all ticket sales donated directly to participating local PTA programs.
  • Sensory-Friendly Mornings: First Sunday of each month with dialed-down music, dimmed lights, and reduced capacity.
  • Youth Sports Sponsorships: Donating gear and court practice slots to local athletic clubs and teams.
