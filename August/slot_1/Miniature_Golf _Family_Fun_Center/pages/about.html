Our Story

Built by parents, golfers, and dreamers to bring world-class entertainment, friendly competition, and pure celebration to families across the region.

Dedicated to Fun Minds

Crafting Joyful Family Adventures Since 2016

PUTT began with a simple yet passionate vision: create an imaginative entertainment haven where adults have just as much fun as children. From four handcrafted miniature golf courses inspired by global landscapes to high-tech arcades and electric racing, every corner is designed to spark creativity, laughter, and playful competition.

Today, our family entertainment center welcomes over 50,000 visitors every year, offering spotless facilities, certified guest safety, and unforgettable memories for all generations.

Mission & Core Values
