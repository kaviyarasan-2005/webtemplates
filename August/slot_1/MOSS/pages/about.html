Cultivated with Intention

For over three decades, MOSS has been a sanctuary for those who seek beauty in imperfection and meaning in patience.

Our Story

Founded in 1989, MOSS was born from a profound respect for the ancient art of bonsai. What began as a modest collection of locally sourced specimens has slowly grown into a sanctuary for cultivators from around the world.

We apply the philosophy of wabi-sabi to everything we do. It is an understanding that true beauty is found in the imperfect, the impermanent, and the incomplete. Every tree in our care is guided by this belief, shaping not just branches, but character.

From our humble beginnings in a single greenhouse, MOSS has evolved into an internationally recognized practice. We continue to honor our roots, tending to each specimen with the same patience and devotion that sparked our journey decades ago.

The Principles We Tend

Patience

True beauty cannot be rushed. We grow specimens that outlive us.

Imperfection

Wabi-sabi teaches us that asymmetry and age carry their own profound elegance.

Harmony

Every element in a garden must speak to every other — stone, tree, water, and light.

Respect for Time

We tend trees that were ancient before we were born, and plant seeds for those unborn.
