Common Inquiries

How often should I water my bonsai?

Watering frequency depends on species, pot size, season and environment. As a general guide, check the soil daily and water when the top centimetre becomes slightly dry. Most indoor species require watering every 2–3 days in summer and less frequently in winter.

What light conditions do bonsai need?

Most species require bright, indirect light for at least 4–6 hours daily. Outdoor species thrive in natural sunlight, while indoor species prefer a bright windowsill away from direct midday sun and cold draughts.

When should I repot my tree?

Repotting is typically performed every 2–5 years, depending on species and age. Young, vigorous trees may need annual repotting, while mature specimens can often be left for 5 or more years. Early spring, before growth begins, is the optimal time.

Do you ship bonsai internationally?

Yes. We ship to select international destinations using climate-controlled packaging designed for live plant transport. We comply with all CITES regulations and phytosanitary requirements. Please contact us for destination-specific information.

What is your returns policy?

If a specimen arrives damaged due to transit, we will arrange a replacement or full refund within 7 days of receipt. We cannot accept returns for trees that show signs of neglect after delivery. All sales of workshop materials and soil are final.

Do you offer gift certificates?

Yes. MOSS gift certificates are available in any denomination and can be applied to any product or service. They make a distinctive gift for collectors and garden enthusiasts alike.
