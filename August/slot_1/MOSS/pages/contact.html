Visitor Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers for planning your visit and acquiring living specimens.

Do I need an advance appointment to visit the nursery?
General garden walks and retail nursery access are open to the public without reservations Tuesday through Sunday. However, if you wish to consult personally with a senior master cultivator regarding bespoke garden design or private acquisitions over £5,000, we recommend requesting a private appointment.
Can live bonsai specimens be exported internationally?
Yes. MOSS manages full international phytosanitary certification, CITES permits, quarantine isolation, and climate-controlled freight to North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. Our concierge team handles all customs documentation.
What is your policy regarding photography and filming?
Personal photography is welcomed throughout our outdoor courtyards and viewing paths. We kindly request that tripod setups and commercial or fashion photography be pre-approved by our press team.
Are workshops and cultivation masterclasses available?
We host seasonal weekend masterclasses covering wiring, repotting, nebari refinement, and winter care. Places are limited to 8 participants per session to ensure individual attention. Select "Cultivation Workshop" in our inquiry form for upcoming schedules.
