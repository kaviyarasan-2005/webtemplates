Master Repotting Service

Entrust your specimen's health to our master cultivators. Professional root assessment, premium soil preparation, and artisan pot selection.

What We Do

Our master cultivators approach repotting as a critical health intervention for your bonsai. We meticulously assess the root structure, pruning away dead or decaying material while preserving the vital feeder roots necessary for your specimen's ongoing vigor.

Every tree receives a custom soil blend, typically combining premium Japanese akadama, pumice, and lava rock, carefully calibrated to the species' moisture and drainage requirements.

  • Root system health assessment before and after
  • Premium akadama and lava rock soil preparation
  • Hand selection of appropriate pot size and material
  • Drainage layer and mesh installation
  • Post-repotting treatment with mycorrhizal inoculant
  • 30-day aftercare protocol documentation
