Keeper Responsibilities

How to Keep Your Guarantee 100% Valid

Live reptiles are sensitive animals requiring prompt reception and precise habitats. Follow these 4 mandatory steps to keep your full warranty active:

1

Setup & Calibrate Habitat 48h in Advance

Your terrarium must be completely set up and running for at least 48 hours prior to arrival. Thermal gradients (basking spot, warm ambient, and cool retreat) and humidity levels must be stabilized and verified using digital probe thermometers or an infrared temperature gun.

2

Receive on Carrier's First Delivery Attempt

You or an authorized adult must be present to sign for and receive the package upon the courier's first attempt (typically between 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM via FedEx Priority Overnight). If held at a FedEx Ship Center, pick up within 4 hours. Packages left on porches void coverage.

3

Immediate Inspection & 2-Hour Notification

Unbox your specimen immediately in a secure, climate-controlled room. Inspect for normal alertness, clear eyes, and uninjured limbs. In the rare event of transit injury or lethargy, email photos of the animal on the shipping label to support@zillpets.com within 2 hours.

4

Observe 48-Hour Quiet Acclimation

Transfer the animal directly into its warm enclosure with fresh water. Do not handle, display to guests, or force feed during the first 48 hours. Acclimation stress is normal; allow your pet to explore its thermal gradient undisturbed before introducing feeding routines.

Logistics Safety

Weather & Safe Shipping Protocols

We never compromise an animal's life for fast dispatch. Every shipment is routed based on real-time temperature forecasts across origin, transit hubs, and your local destination.

45°F – 88°F: Standard Doorstep Delivery

Safe for direct FedEx Priority Overnight delivery to your residential or business address. Signature required on first delivery attempt by 10:30 AM.

35°F–45°F or 88°F–95°F: Hub Pickup Required

Packages are routed to a certified, climate-controlled FedEx Ship Center counter. This prevents animals from riding in unconditioned delivery trucks. Pick up within 4 hours.

< 35°F or > 95°F: Safety Hold

Dispatches are paused automatically during severe blizzards, heatwaves, or airport hub freezes. Your animal remains comfortably cared for in our facility until safe conditions return.

What Voids the Health Guarantee

To ensure fairness and promote animal welfare, the following actions or husbandry errors will void both the Live-Arrival and 7-Day Health Guarantee:

❌ Unregulated Heat Devices Thermal burns caused by heat rocks or heat mats without digital probe thermostats.
❌ Loose Calcium Sand Substrate Fatal intestinal impactions caused by commercial "Vita-Sand" or crushed walnut shells in juveniles.
❌ Incompatible Cohabitation Stress or bite injuries caused by housing solitary species together (bearded dragons, ball pythons).
❌ Post-Unboxing Trauma or Escapes Drops, crushing injuries, stepped-on tails, attacks by family pets, or escapes from unlatched lids.
❌ Gecko Tail Drops (Autotomy) Tail drops occurring after safe delivery. This is a natural defense reflex; "frog butt" geckos thrive completely normally.
❌ Wild-Caught Insect Feeding Lethal pesticide poisoning or internal parasite transmissions from yard-collected insects.
Claim Filing

How to Submit a Guarantee Claim

Our claims process is straightforward and handled directly by our senior veterinary staff. Here is what to do if you encounter an issue:

1

Notice Window

Email support@zillpets.com within 2 hours of delivery for DOA/transit concerns, or within 7 days for health concerns. Include your Order #.

2

Photo Evidence

Attach 3 clear photos/videos: (1) specimen showing condition, (2) courier shipping label on box, (3) enclosure showing digital temperature and humidity gauges.

3

Vet Review

Our exotic husbandry directors and veterinary technicians review your case documentation within 12–24 business hours to determine the best resolution.

4

Prompt Resolution

Approved claims receive an immediate replacement animal of equal or greater value, or full non-expiring store credit applied to your ZILL account.

Legal Terms & Husbandry FAQ

Full Policy Terms & Conditions

Click any section below to review the comprehensive terms of sale, carrier conditions, and replacement protocols.

All captive-bred reptiles are backed by our dual-layer guarantee: (1) 100% Live-Arrival upon initial delivery, and (2) an extended 7-Day Health Guarantee (168 hours from delivery timestamp). If your animal shows persistent signs of distress, illness, or genetic defects within this period despite correct husbandry, we will replace the animal or issue store credit.

To maintain guarantee validity, the customer (or a designated adult) must be physically present to receive and sign for the shipment on the carrier's first delivery attempt (typically by 10:30 AM via FedEx Priority Overnight). If held at a certified FedEx Ship Center, the package must be picked up within 4 hours of arrival. Packages left on unattended porches in ambient sun or cold void live-arrival protection.

In the extremely rare event that an animal arrives deceased or in critical condition, notice must be emailed to support@zillpets.com within strictly 2 hours of the carrier's recorded delivery timestamp. Submissions must include: (1) clear photographs or video of the animal resting on the shipping box with the shipping label fully legible, and (2) your order number.

The 7-Day Health Guarantee requires that the buyer has prepared a species-appropriate habitat adhering to the environmental metrics in ZILL Care Guides (correct thermal gradient, linear UVB where applicable, and humidity). In the event of a health claim, the buyer must provide clear photos of the enclosure showing digital thermometer and hygrometer readings.

The guarantee does not cover: (1) physical injuries sustained after unboxing (falls, stepped-on limbs, escapes), (2) thermal burns caused by unregulated heat rocks or mats without probe thermostats, (3) intestinal impactions caused by calcium sand or crushed walnut shells in juveniles, (4) tail drops (autotomy) in geckos occurring post-unboxing, (5) housing with other reptiles, or (6) feeding wild-caught insects.

Live reptiles ship exclusively via FedEx Priority Overnight Monday through Wednesday to avoid weekend warehouse holds. Shipments are dispatched only when origin, hub, and destination temperatures are between 38°F and 92°F. Between 32°F–38°F or 92°F–98°F, shipping is restricted to FedEx Ship Center counter pickup. We hold shipments automatically during extreme weather.

Live feeder insects (Dubia roaches, crickets, hornworms, BSFL) are guaranteed 95%+ viable upon arrival. If transit delays cause mortality exceeding 5%, submit photos of the shipping box and count within 24 hours of delivery for a free reshipment or store credit.

Approved live-animal claims are resolved via an immediate replacement specimen of equivalent or higher value, or full store credit for the animal's purchase price. Store credit never expires and can be used on any reptile, habitat, or supply order. Courier shipping fees are non-refundable.
