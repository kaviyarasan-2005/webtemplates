Featured: Bearded Dragon

Mastering the Outback Habitat

Bearded dragons are incredibly resilient and full of personality, but they require a very specific setup to mimic their native Australian environment. From high-output UVB lighting to the perfect basking gradient, getting it right is crucial to their long-term vitality.

"The most common mistake new keepers make is underestimating the need for intense, quality UVB. It's not just light—it's literal life support for a bearded dragon."
