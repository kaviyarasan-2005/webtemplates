Common Questions Before You Inquire

Quick answers to our most frequent keeper, species, product, and health guarantee questions.

Our online catalog displays currently available captive-bred specimens. If a species or morph is marked "Waitlist" or you are seeking a specific lineage (such as Tangerine Leopard Geckos or Banana Ball Pythons), select "Animal Availability" in the enquiry form above. We maintain active waiting lists and accept 20% fully refundable deposits to hold hatchlings once veterinary clearance is complete.

Submit an enquiry under "Care Advice" with your vivarium dimensions, heating elements, UVB bulb specifications, and digital thermometer/hygrometer readings. We will verify your thermal gradients and basking zones to ensure a 100% stress-free transition for your animal.

Yes. Our Bioactive Tropical and Arid Outback kits include professional-grade enclosures, drainage mesh, bioactive substrate, microfauna cleanup crews (springtails and isopods), certified lighting fixtures, and live reptile-safe botanical starters. Inquire under "Products" if you need a custom-dimensioned enclosure or tailored supply bundle.

For DOA or severe transit distress, email guarantee@zillpets.com or call our 24/7 hotline (+1 234 567-891) within 2 hours of delivery with photos of the animal on the courier box. For health concerns arising within the 7-day extended guarantee, submit an enquiry under "Health Guarantee" with your Order # and habitat gauge photos. Our veterinary directors will review and provide immediate medical guidance, replacement, or store credit.
