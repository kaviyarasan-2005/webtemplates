Our Story

From a Garage Dream to Toronto's Forge

Co-founders Marcus Webb and Tara Ng both competed at the national level in axe throwing leagues — and they both saw the same gap in Toronto's market. There was nowhere that offered a truly professional, safety-first experience for new throwers and serious competitors alike. In 2019, they signed a lease on a reclaimed industrial warehouse in Toronto's west end and got to work building Axe Forge from scratch.

Since opening day, Axe Forge has welcomed over 12,000 throwers through its doors — from birthday groups and corporate teams to NATF league competitors chasing gold. In 2022, demand was so high that Marcus and Tara expanded with a second lane block, bringing the venue to 10 full throwing lanes. Today, Axe Forge is recognized as one of Canada's top dedicated axe throwing facilities — and it all started with a bold idea and a whole lot of grit.

Our Core Values

🎯

Safety First

Every session begins with a certified safety briefing. Our lanes meet NATF standards with full protective barriers and trained coaches watching every throw.

🌍

Radical Inclusion

Axe throwing is for everyone — first-timers, seasoned veterans, people of all abilities. We meet you where you are and celebrate every bullseye equally.

🏆

Competitive Excellence

We host NATF-sanctioned leagues and tournaments, pushing the limits of skill and competitive spirit for throwers at every level of the game.

🤝

Community First

We're more than a venue. We've built a community of regulars, coaches, and champions who keep coming back — and who bring their friends.

♻️

Sustainable Operations

Our axes are Canadian-forged, our wood targets are sustainably sourced, and we compost all target shavings. We care about what we leave behind.

⚡

Relentless Innovation

From Cyber Glow UV nights to digital score tracking, we never stop finding new ways to elevate the experience and keep things fresh for our community.
