FAQs

Got Questions?

Do I need prior experience?
Absolutely not. Our coaches will walk you through grip, stance, and release. You'll be throwing like a pro within minutes — no experience needed at all.
What's the minimum age?
Participants must be 12 years or older. Under-18s must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian who signs the waiver.
What should I wear?
Closed-toe shoes are mandatory. Wear comfortable clothes you can move in freely. All throwing equipment is provided by us.
Can I bring alcohol?
Outside alcohol is not permitted. We have a licensed bar on-site. Anyone showing signs of intoxication will be removed from the throwing area.
How many people per lane?
Up to 6 active throwers per lane, with spectators welcome. For larger groups, we offer multi-lane packages for 200+ guests.
What's your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your session. Within 24 hours, we offer a 100% credit toward a future booking. No-shows forfeit their deposit.
