Behind the Counter

At POPZ, every flavor begins with a story. Our founder, Elena Rodriguez, started with a single copper kettle and a passion for transforming the humble popcorn kernel into something extraordinary.

Today, our team of artisan confectioners handcrafts every batch in our Sweet Haven workshop, using locally sourced ingredients and time-honored techniques passed down through three generations.
