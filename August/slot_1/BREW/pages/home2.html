Hop Varieties of the Month

This Month's Selection

We source the freshest hops from premier growing regions around the world. Each month our team curates a selection of varieties at peak freshness, selected for their aromatic complexity and brewing versatility.

Whether you are designing a bold West Coast IPA or a delicate session ale, these four hops give you a complete flavor palette to work with.

  • Cascade Floral and citrus notes with moderate bitterness. A classic American hop for pale ales.
  • Citra Intense tropical fruit and grapefruit. High alpha acid for bold, juicy IPAs.
  • Mosaic Complex berry, tropical, and earthy layers. Perfect for hazy and New England styles.
  • Simcoe Pine and passion fruit character. Versatile dual-use hop for deep aroma complexity.
