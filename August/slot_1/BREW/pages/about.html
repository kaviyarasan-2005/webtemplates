Rooted in Craft

Our Story

From a Garage Workshop to a Community of Brewers

BREW Home Brewing Supply began in the spring of 2015, when a small group of friends turned a weekend hobby in a Portland garage into a mission — to put the finest brewing ingredients and knowledge within reach of every home brewer in the Pacific Northwest.

Founded with a Purpose

Jake Morrison spent years home brewing in his kitchen, experimenting with grain bills, hop schedules, and yeast strains. Frustrated by the lack of a dedicated, knowledgeable supply shop, he built one with partner Sarah Chen — and more than fifty curious brewers showed up before noon on opening day.

Grown into a Community Hub

What began as a single storefront now offers hands-on brewing classes, monthly meetups, and nationwide shipping of carefully sourced malts, hops, yeasts, and equipment — every product hand-picked to the standards we demand for our own brews.

Great beer starts with great ingredients and a passionate community. Whether you are brewing your first batch or perfecting a recipe refined over decades, BREW is here to support your journey at every step.

What We Stand For

Quality Ingredients

Every malt, hop, and yeast we carry is evaluated for freshness, purity, and performance, so every brew starts from a foundation of excellence.

Sustainable Sourcing

We partner with suppliers who use regenerative farming and sustainable packaging — from solar-powered malting to compostable grain bags.

Community First

BREW is a gathering place for brewers of every level — hosting brew nights, charity brew days, and local homebrew competitions.

Brewer Education

From beginner all-grain workshops to advanced yeast seminars, our classes are taught by certified brewing professionals.

Trusted Suppliers

Briess Malt

Premium North American maltster known for consistent base and specialty malts.

Yakima Valley Hops

Pacific Northwest growers supplying fresh aroma and bittering hops like Cascade, Citra, and Mosaic.

White Labs

Industry-standard liquid yeast with a wide range of ale, lager, and wild strains.

Fermentis

Global dry-yeast leader delivering reliable, high-performing fermentation.

Weyermann

German malt house with over a century of tradition, famed for specialty malts and smoked rauchmalz.

Great Western

Trusted Pacific Northwest supplier known for consistent two-row base malts.

Meet the Team

Jake Morrison

Founder & Head Brewer

A homebrewer since 2006, Jake turned his garage hobby into Portland’s most trusted brewing supply shop. He holds a Certificate in Brewing Science from the Siebel Institute and has judged at the Great American Beer Festival.

Sarah Chen

Operations Manager

Sarah keeps BREW running like a well-tuned brewhouse. With a background in supply chain management and a passion for craft lagers, she ensures every order ships on time and every customer leaves with a smile.

Tom Baker

Lead Instructor

Tom has taught over 500 brewing workshops at BREW. A former professional brewer with stints at two award-winning Oregon breweries, he specializes in helping beginners make their first all-grain batch with confidence.

Lisa Park

Ingredient Specialist

Lisa is the palate behind BREW’s curated ingredient selection. With a degree in Food Science and a love for hop-forward IPAs, she travels to farms and malt houses each season to source the freshest ingredients available.
