Rooted in Craft

Our Story

BREW Home Brewing Supply was born in the spring of 2015 in a modest garage workshop on the east side of Portland, Oregon. What began as a weekend hobby between a small group of friends quickly grew into something much bigger — a shared dream of bringing the finest brewing ingredients and knowledge to every home brewer in the Pacific Northwest.

Our founder, Jake Morrison, had spent years home brewing in his kitchen, experimenting with grain bills, hop schedules, and yeast strains. Frustrated by the lack of a dedicated, knowledgeable local supply shop, he decided to build one himself. With support from his partner Sarah Chen, the first BREW storefront opened its doors on a quiet Saturday morning, and over fifty curious brewers showed up before noon.

Over the years, BREW has grown from a single retail location into a thriving community hub. We now offer hands-on brewing classes, host monthly meetups, and ship premium ingredients to home brewers across the country. Our shelves are stocked with carefully sourced malts, hops, yeasts, and equipment — every product hand-picked to meet the standards we would demand for our own brews.

At our core, we believe that great beer starts with great ingredients and a passionate community. Whether you are brewing your very first batch or perfecting a recipe you have refined over decades, BREW is here to support your journey every step of the way.

What We Stand For

Quality Ingredients

Every malt, hop, and yeast strain we carry is rigorously evaluated for freshness, purity, and performance. We partner only with suppliers who share our uncompromising standards, so every brew you make with BREW ingredients starts from a foundation of excellence.

Sustainable Sourcing

We actively seek out suppliers who employ regenerative farming practices and sustainable packaging. From solar-powered malting facilities to compostable grain bags, we consider the environmental footprint of every product on our shelves.

Community First

BREW is more than a store — it is a gathering place for brewers of all skill levels. We host monthly brew nights, organize charity brew days, and sponsor local homebrew competitions because we believe the best beer is brewed among friends.

Brewer Education

From beginner all-grain workshops to advanced yeast management seminars, our class calendar is designed to keep you learning and growing. Our instructors are certified brewing professionals who are just as passionate about teaching as they are about brewing.

Meet the Team

Jake Morrison

Founder & Head Brewer

A homebrewer since 2006, Jake turned his garage hobby into Portland’s most trusted brewing supply shop. He holds a Certificate in Brewing Science from the Siebel Institute and has judged at the Great American Beer Festival.

Sarah Chen

Operations Manager

Sarah keeps BREW running like a well-tuned brewhouse. With a background in supply chain management and a passion for craft lagers, she ensures every order ships on time and every customer leaves with a smile.

Tom Baker

Lead Instructor

Tom has taught over 500 brewing workshops at BREW. A former professional brewer with stints at two award-winning Oregon breweries, he specializes in helping beginners make their first all-grain batch with confidence.

Lisa Park

Ingredient Specialist

Lisa is the palate behind BREW’s curated ingredient selection. With a degree in Food Science and a love for hop-forward IPAs, she travels to farms and malt houses each season to source the freshest ingredients available.
