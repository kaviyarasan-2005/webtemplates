Premium Grain Mill

About This Product

The Premium Grain Mill from BREW is engineered for the home brewer who demands consistency, durability, and precision. Built from 304 stainless steel throughout, this mill delivers a uniform crush batch after batch, giving you the efficiency and extract potential that serious brewers require.

Whether you are brewing a light American lager or a heavy Russian imperial stout, the adjustable roller gap lets you dial in the perfect crush for your grain bill and brewing system. The hand crank operates smoothly with minimal effort, and the motorize-ready design means you can upgrade to a powered drive whenever you are ready.

  • Adjustable gap range from 0.025 to 0.1 inches
  • Stainless steel construction throughout
  • 5lb hopper capacity
  • Easy-clean design
  • Compatible with all grain types

Technical Details

Material 304 Stainless Steel
Hopper Capacity 5 lbs (2.27 kg)
Gap Range 0.025" - 0.1"
Roller Diameter 1.25" (31.75mm)
Dimensions 12" x 8" x 14"
Weight 12 lbs (5.4 kg)
Drive Manual Hand Crank (motorize-ready)
Warranty 5 Years
