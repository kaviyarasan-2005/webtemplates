THE GYM

Auto-Belays

Sixteen Trublue auto-belay units across our top-rope section. No partner needed — solo climbing is always on.

Moon Board

A 40-degree adjustable Moon Board with the full Moonclimbing hold set. Train specific movement patterns from V4 to V12.

Campus Board

Full-length campus board with rungs from 18mm to 35mm. Essential for explosive contact strength and lock-off power.
