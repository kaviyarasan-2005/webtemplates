What to Expect

COURSE OVERVIEW

The CRAG Lead Climbing Course is a 3-hour intensive program covering everything you need to confidently lead climb indoors. Led by AMGA-certified instructors, this course is designed for top-rope climbers ready to take their climbing to the next level.

Upon completion, you will receive a CRAG Lead Certification card valid at any affiliated gym. No previous lead experience required — just a basic level of climbing fitness and the desire to progress.

  • Rope systems and lead-specific knots
  • Reading a route and clip planning
  • Falling technique and safe fall practice
  • Belay commands and communication
  • Clipping efficiency and body position
  • Post-course lead belay test included
Full Breakdown

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Eight focused modules delivered by a single AMGA-certified instructor in a small-group setting.

Knot Tying Figure-8 follow-through, stopper knots, backup knots
Belay Commands Standard lead belay commands and communication protocols
Falling Technique Safe falling, push-off technique, avoiding wall contact
Route Reading Clip sequencing, rest positions, crux analysis
Clipping Efficiency Rope orientation, gate-side clips, backclip prevention
Rope Management Avoiding leg loops, slack management, rope flaking
Safety Checks Pre-climb partner checks and final safety protocols
Post-Course Test Practical lead belay assessment with an instructor
