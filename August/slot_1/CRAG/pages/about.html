Our Story

OUR STORY

In 2018, two lifelong climbers — Elena Marsh and Jamie Cortez — walked into an abandoned warehouse on the edge of Boulder with nothing but a vision and a truck full of climbing holds. The building smelled of machine oil and ambition. They smelled of chalk.

What followed was 18 months of weekend builds, maxed-out credit cards, and the occasional bloodied knuckle. Every panel was cut, drilled, and painted by hand. The first members were friends who showed up not just to climb, but to help set routes in exchange for free day passes.

Today, CRAG hosts over 1,200 active members, runs certified instructor programs, and trains competition climbers from across the region. But the warehouse spirit never left. You can still feel it in the chalk-dusted air.

THE CRAG CODE

The values that guide every hold we set and every route we open.

Safety First

Every decision starts with keeping our community safe. Mandatory orientations, certified instructors, and continuous equipment inspection.

Inclusive Grading

Our grading is transparent and consistent. No sand-bagging, no ego. V-grades you can trust.

Community Over Competition

We cheer each other on. From your first V0 to your hardest project — every send matters here.

Leave No Trace

Sustainable practices in everything we do: chalk management, energy efficiency, and zero-waste initiatives.
