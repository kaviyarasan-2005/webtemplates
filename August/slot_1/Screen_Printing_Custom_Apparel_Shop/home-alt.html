Our Techniques

Ink & Print Methods

Every method mastered for the perfect finish

01

Plastisol Ink

Industry standard — vibrant, opaque, durable on dark fabrics. Perfect for bold designs.

02

Water-Based Ink

Soft hand feel that breathes — eco-friendly with a vintage, worn-in look.

03

Discharge Print

Removes garment dye and replaces with color — no ink layer, ultra-soft feel.

04

DTG Printing

Direct-to-garment for photo-quality detail — ideal for complex multi-color artwork.

05

Heat Transfer

Vinyl cut & pressed — great for names, numbers, and small batch custom pieces.

FAQ

Common Questions

Our minimum order is 25 pieces for screen printing. For DTG and heat transfer, we can accommodate orders as small as 1 piece, though per-unit pricing is higher for smaller runs.
We accept AI, EPS, PDF, PSD, PNG (300+ DPI), and SVG files. For best results, submit vector artwork. Our team can also help convert your files — just ask!
Standard turnaround is 7 business days from proof approval. Rush options available: 5-day priority and 48-hour express. Delivery time depends on shipping method chosen.
Yes! Our in-house design team can help create or refine your artwork. Basic design assistance is included on orders of 100+ pieces. Custom design projects are quoted separately — see our pricing page for details.
We guarantee quality on every order. If there are any defects in printing or if the order doesn't match the approved proof, we'll reprint at no charge. Contact us within 7 days of delivery.
