Case Studies

Tech Conference

CloudCon 2025

The Challenge: A massive tech summit needed 5,000 custom tees in 4 distinct colors printed and shipped across 3 different venues nationwide, with only a 5-day lead time.

The Solution: We fired up three automatic M&R presses simultaneously, running shifts overnight to hit the tight deadline. We handled all the logistics, dropping the boxes directly at the loading docks of the convention centers the day before the event.

"Seamless from proof to delivery. PRNT saved our event when our previous vendor backed out." — CloudCon Events Team

Corporate Uniforms

NovaTech Annual Rebrand

The Challenge: NovaTech underwent a complete brand refresh and needed their entire 800-person workforce outfitted in new uniforms within 3 weeks, requiring strict adherence to their new Pantone colors.

The Solution: We sourced premium performance polos and utilized our multi-head embroidery machines for 800 left-chest logos. Every single piece was poly-bagged and labeled by department and size, making distribution for NovaTech's HR team effortless.

"PRNT understood our brand standards better than we did. The embroidery is flawless." — NovaTech HR
