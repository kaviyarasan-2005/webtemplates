03 — Choosing Your Cigar

Choosing Your Cigar

New to cigars? Three things matter more than price — strength, size and what you drink alongside. Here's how to start.

Strength

Start mild. A medium-bodied cigar in the afternoon is where most palates begin — the strength meter on each card is your guide.

Format

Longer cigars smoke cooler and slower; a shorter Robusto is a perfect 45-minute introduction. Ask us and we'll match the size to your evening.

Pairing

Rum, single malt or espresso each transform a cigar. Our bar is stocked to pair — browse the Bar & Spirits menu.

THE NOIR HUMIDOR

Kept Perfect, So You Taste More

Our walk-in humidor is held at a constant 69% relative humidity and 18°C — the conditions that let a cigar age gracefully. Here's how to look after yours at home.

A sealed humidor with a 69% Boveda pack is the simplest reliable setup. Keep it out of direct sun and away from radiators — heat does more damage than dry air.

65–70% is the sweet spot. Below 60% cigars dry out and burn hot; above 75% they swell, draw poorly and can develop mould.

Many improve for years. As a rule of thumb, aged cigars round off their edges and gain notes of leather and dried fruit. We rest our stock for a minimum of 30 days before they reach the shelf.

It's a matter of taste. Cellophane slows the exchange of humidity and protects the wrapper; leaving it on is perfectly fine and lets cigars settle at their own pace.
