Cuban Heritage

Cohiba & The Havana Legacy

Since 1966, Cohiba has represented the pinnacle of Cuban tobacco craftsmanship. Originally created for Fidel Castro, these cigars are hand-rolled by the most skilled torcedores in La Habana. Each leaf is triple-fermented in a proprietary process unique to Cohiba, producing an unparalleled smoothness and complexity that has defined luxury cigars for six decades.

Dominican Excellence

Davidoff: Where Precision Meets Art

Born in Geneva, perfected in the Dominican Republic. Davidoff's philosophy of total control — from seed to smoke — means every cigar undergoes a five-year aging process before reaching your hands. Their Millennium Blend represents the apex of this obsession, a smooth yet complex creation that rewards the patient smoker.

The Art of Humidor Care

Expert preservation guidelines to maintain your collection at peak smoking condition.

Premium cigars should be stored at 65–72% relative humidity. NOIR's humidors are calibrated daily to 70% RH to ensure optimal preservation. Too high, and cigars risk mold; too low, and they dry out — losing their complex flavor profile irreversibly.

A new humidor requires a 5–7 day seasoning period before introducing cigars. Place a small dish of distilled water inside and wipe the cedar interior lightly with a damp cloth. Never use tap water — mineral deposits affect the cedar's performance.

With proper care, premium cigars improve with age — much like fine wine. A well-maintained humidor can preserve cigars for decades. Our vintage collection includes Cuban cigars from the 1990s that are only now reaching their ideal smoking window.
