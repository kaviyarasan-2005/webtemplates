01 — The Philosophy

Pairing Is Everything

A great cigar is half the ritual. The other half is what rests beside it. Our cellar is small on purpose — each bottle earned its place by complementing smoke, whether with sweetness, spice or simply a long, clean finish.

Ask a host for a recommendation and we will match the cigar to the glass: a Speyside with something creamy, an Islay with something full. Not sure? Start with a flight and taste the difference for yourself.

03 — The Pairing Guide

What to Drink with Your Cigar

Simple starting points. Our hosts are always happy to go deeper.

Mild & CreamyChampagne, light rum, aged tawny port
Medium & BalancedSpeyside single malt, cognac, Barolo
Full & SpicyIslay whisky, aged rum, Pedro Ximénez
Any Strength · No AlcoholSyphon coffee, pairing tea, botanical serves

Match the Weight

Bolder cigars stand up to bolder drinks. If the smoke is full-bodied, reach for something with equal weight and sweetness.

Mind the Sugar

A touch of sweetness — sherry, port, aged rum — softens spice and pairs beautifully with leather and cedar notes.

Ask Your Host

There are no wrong answers. Tell us what you enjoy and we will build the pairing around your cigar, not the other way around.
