02 — Your Visit

Good to Know

A few house rules and comforts so your evening runs exactly as it should.

Dress Code

Smart casual to black tie. We ask guests to dress in keeping with a private members' lounge — sportswear is not permitted.

Age Policy

NOIR is strictly over-18. Proof of age may be requested, and we reserve the right to refuse entry at our discretion.

Facilities

Walk-in humidor, licensed bar and terrace, private den and humidor lockers. Freedom smoke — chosen from our humidor.
