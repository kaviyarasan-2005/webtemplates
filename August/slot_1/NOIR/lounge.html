01 — Welcome

An Evening of Distinction

Nestled in the heart of Mayfair, NOIR is more than a cigar lounge — it is a sanctuary for those who appreciate the art of slow living. Our walk-in humidor houses over 300 hand-selected cigars from the world's most esteemed tobacco regions.

Every visit is a ritual. From the moment you arrive, our concierge guides your selection, pairing your chosen cigar with the finest single malts, aged rums and specialty coffees from our curated cellar. Come as a guest; leave as a regular.

03 — Facilities

Everything You Need for a Perfect Evening

Walk-in Humidor

300+ cigars across Cuba, Nicaragua, the Dominican Republic and Honduras, kept at a perfect 70°F / 70%.

Full Bar & Cellar

Single malts, aged rums, cognac & armagnac, wine and signature cocktails — plus barista coffee and non-alcoholic pairings.

Private Dens

Two bookable rooms with independent ventilation for meetings, celebrations or simply a quieter corner.

Heated Terrace

An open-air terrace with heaters and blankets, open year-round for those who prefer the night air.

Concierge Service

A dedicated host to guide your selection, arrange pairings and remember your preferences on every visit.

Lockers & Coat Care

Complimentary coat service and members' humidor lockers to store your personal collection between visits.

04 — New Here?

Your First Visit, Made Simple

Never smoked before, or just new to NOIR? There is no dress rehearsal. Here is exactly how an evening unfolds.

Step 01

Reserve & Arrive

Book a table online in under a minute, then let us know your party size and any occasion. A host will greet you at reception.

Step 02

Meet Your Cigar

We ask a few questions about taste, strength and time available, then walk you through the humidor to find your match.

Step 03

Pair & Relax

Your host suggests a drink to complement the cigar — whisky, rum, wine, coffee or something alcohol-free. Then the evening is yours.

Step 04

Return When Ready

No pressure to join. If you enjoy the evening, memberships unlock priority tables, lockers and members-only tastings.
