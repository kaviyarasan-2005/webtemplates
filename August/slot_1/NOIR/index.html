The Lounge Atmosphere

An Evening of Distinction

Nestled in the heart of Mayfair, NOIR is more than a cigar lounge — it is a sanctuary for those who appreciate the art of slow living. Our humidor houses over 300 hand-selected cigars from the world's most esteemed tobacco regions.

Every visit is a ritual. From the moment you enter, our expert concierge guides your selection, pairing your chosen cigar with the finest single malts, aged rums, and specialty coffees from our curated cellar.
