01 — The Experience

More Than a Cigar

NOIR is not a shop with chairs. It is a lounge built for the whole occasion — a walk-in humidor of over 300 cigars, a bar of carefully paired spirits, deep leather seating and hosts who treat every guest like a regular.

Arrive for the cigar, stay for the company. Whether you are a seasoned collector or lighting your very first, the evening is designed to feel unhurried, warm and entirely yours.

04 — The Bar

Spirits Worth Staying For

A cigar is only half the ritual. Our cellar pairs single malts, aged rums, cognac, wine and signature cocktails with the humidor — plus barista coffee and alcohol-free serves for those who prefer them.

Not sure where to start? Ask for a flight and taste your way through the pairing guide our hosts have built over the years.
