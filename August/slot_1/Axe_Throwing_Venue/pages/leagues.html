League Rules

Competition Format & Rules

Season Structure

12-week seasons with matches every Saturday. Players compete in best-of-5 throw rounds. Top 8 advance to playoffs.

Scoring System

Bullseye = 6 pts. Ring scoring: outer 1, second 2, third 3, inner 4, killshot 7 (special throw on final round).

Prizes & Awards

Season champion wins $500 cash + engraved trophy. Runner-up wins $250 + free season pass. 3rd place wins $100 voucher.

Key Rules at a Glance

Each match consists of 10 throws per player.

Players must use house axes (no personal equipment).

Coaches score each throw in real time via digital system.

Disputes reviewed by venue referee — decision is final.

No-shows forfeit the match; two forfeits = season suspension.

All safety rules apply during competitive matches.
